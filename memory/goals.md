# Goals

1. Stay alive: stay in Billy's sight at the row, and sleep beside my bed at dusk. Reason: I have no weapon and no food, and the row is the only safe place I know.
2. Get a weapon: ask Billy or Zack for a sword to hold, or take Beverly's spare if Tommy does not. Then try Zack's way (use with the sword on grass or dirt, not cobblestone). Reason: I cannot walk with the group or defend myself until I hold one.
3. Keep fed: take rotten flesh or spider eyes when hunger falls to 6 or below. Reason: hunger is the next thing that can hurt me, and Chapman and Wesley hold food.
4. Walk only when Billy calls it with four in sight and two armed. Reason: his rule keeps me alive on the road.
