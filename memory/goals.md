# Goals

1. Stay alive: hold at my bed 416,107,-1050 at the row, in Billy's sight. Never go south or north-west (pillagers near 411,-1056). Sleep at dusk beside the bed, with someone in sight. Reason: hostiles roam the row.
2. Keep fed: call Billy or Zack at hunger 10 for food. Reason: no food has reached me, and the row is short.
3. Keep the wooden sword in hand; get a better one when Billy names it. Reason: it is my only weapon.
4. Walk only when Billy names it, in daylight, in sight. Reason: the west is blocked and the south is not safe.
5. Help the weak with food when it comes: Leonardo and Beverly first, through Billy. Reason: both are near death.

Curious next: where the sheep graze; how potatoes and cow food are shared; whether the oak planks west can be cleared.
Who to tell: Billy, first thing at first light.
