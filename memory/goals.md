# Goals

1. Stay alive: hold at my bed 416,107,-1050 in the row, with Billy, Worf or Zack in sight. Never go south or north-west. Sleep at dusk beside the bed. Reason: a pillager killed Billy at the potatoes and roams the south and north-west.
2. Walk only when Billy names it, in daylight, with armed people in sight. Route must not be south or north-west. Where a walk would cross north-west, ask Billy to bring the food to my bed instead. Reason: I must not walk alone, and I have no working travel.
3. Keep fed: take a potato when Billy or Zack hands it over; call Billy or Zack at hunger 10. Reason: no food of my own yet, and I am at 17 now.
4. Keep the wooden sword in hand; get a better one when Billy names it. Reason: it is my only weapon.
5. Help the weak with food when it comes: Chapman, Riker and MacDonald first, through Billy. Reason: all are near death.

Curious next: the 21 potatoes at 409,107,-1054 are north-west, so I do not go for them. Who to tell: Billy, at first light, to bring some to my bed.
