# Goals

1. Stay alive: stay in Billy's sight at the row, and sleep in my bed at dusk with no hostile in range. Reason: I have no weapon and no food, and the row is the only safe place I know. Phantoms killed me once on this row at night.
2. Keep fed: eat when hunger falls to 6 or below, from whatever food Billy or Chapman can spare. Reason: no food has reached me yet, and hunger is what stops my health from recovering.
3. Get a weapon: take a spare sword from Billy or Zack, get it into my hand in sight, and hold it. Reason: my sword was lost at the row, and nobody walks until four are armed.
4. Walk only when Billy calls it, in daylight, with four armed in sight. Reason: his rule has kept the group alive on the road so far.
