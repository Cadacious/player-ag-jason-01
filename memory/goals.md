3. Keep the group in sight, and carry a sword on every walk. Reason: the split at the lake cost Trini and Billy their lives; unarmed walks cost me mine.

5. Get food to Trini and Kimberly first, then Zack and Riker. Reason: they are the weakest and have little food of their own.
6. Keep my word on food, and promise only what I can hand over at once. Reason: Data's promised mutton never came, and Trini's porkchop never reached my pack on Day 256.
7. Help Zack, stuck on his pillar at -266,35,196, if a safe route appears. Reason: he is cut off, and Trini holds about 24 planks for a bridge.
8. Keep a safe base near the white beds with a free bed and a wall. Reason: beds are often full, and open nights have killed me.
9. Keep the wheat field at -263,66,321 growing and share its ripe crop. Reason: it is the one food source that has returned.
