# Goals

1. Stay alive: hold at my bed 415,107,-1051 in Billy's sight; at dusk sleep in my bed, or where I stand in the row if the bed refuses, with no hostile in range. Reason: hostiles roam the row at night.
2. Keep fed: eat when hunger is 6 or below. Ask Billy by name for food when he names the call. Reason: no food has reached me yet.
3. Keep a sword in hand, and get a better one when Billy names it. Reason: the wooden sword is the only one I have.
4. Walk only when Billy names it, in daylight, in his sight. Reason: he leads the group and his calls keep us together.
