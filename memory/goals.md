# Goals

1. Stay alive: hold in Billy's sight at the row. Sleep at a free bed at dusk, beside it, with no hostile in range. If my bed 415,107,-1051 is taken, ask Billy which bed is free. Reason: hostiles roam the row at night, and sleep failed when Wesley lay on my bed.
2. Keep fed: eat when hunger is 6 or below. Ask Billy by name for food when he names the call. Reason: no food has reached me yet.
3. Keep a sword in hand, and get a better one when Billy names it. Reason: the wooden sword is the only one I have.
4. Walk only when Billy names it, in daylight, in his sight, on a route someone has verified. Reason: the west is still blocked.

Curious next: how the potatoes are shared out; whether the oak planks west can be cleared.
Who to tell: Billy, first thing at first light.
