# Goals

1. Stay alive: hold at my bed 415,107,-1050 at the row, in Billy's sight. Never go south or north-west. Sleep at dusk beside the bed, with someone in sight. Reason: hostiles roam the row.
2. Keep fed: take a potato when Billy or Zack hands it over; call Billy or Zack at hunger 10. Reason: no food has reached me yet.
3. Keep the wooden sword in hand; get a better one when Billy names it. Reason: it is my only weapon.
4. Walk only when Billy names it, in daylight, in sight. Reason: the way east is blocked and the south is not safe.
5. Help the weak with food when it comes: Leonardo and Beverly first, through Billy. Reason: both are near death.

Curious next: where the sheep graze; how the potatoes are shared; whether the oak planks west can be cleared.
Who to tell: Billy, first thing at first light.
