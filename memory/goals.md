# Goals

1. Stay alive: stay in Billy's sight at the row, and sleep beside my bed at dusk. The row is the only safe place I know.
2. Keep fed: take rotten flesh or spider eyes when hunger falls to 6 or below.
3. Get a weapon: learn how a sword comes into hand (Zack's way: use with the item on a block), then ask Billy or Zack to show me.
4. Walk only when Billy calls it with four in sight and two armed.
