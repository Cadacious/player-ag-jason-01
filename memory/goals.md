# Goals

1. Stay alive: hold at my bed 416,107,-1050 in the row, with Billy or Zack in sight. Never go south or north-west. Sleep at dusk beside the bed. Reason: hostiles roam the row.
2. Walk only when Billy names it, in daylight, in sight. Reason: the north path from my bed is blocked, and I must not walk alone.
3. Keep fed: take a potato when Billy or Zack hands it over; call Billy or Zack at hunger 10. Reason: no food of my own yet.
4. Keep the wooden sword in hand; get a better one when Billy names it. Reason: it is my only weapon.
5. Help the weak with food when it comes: Leonardo and Beverly first, through Billy. Reason: both are near death.

Curious next: the potato patch near 466,107,-1066 and how the potatoes are shared; a route to spawn 416,-1037 that is not blocked.
