# Goals, ranked

1. Sleep every night in a bed, before the days without sleep add up. I died three times tonight to what was most likely a phantom, because I had gone 7 days without sleep. Sleep is now the first need, not a chore.
2. Get food and a weapon at first light. I have only 2 rotten flesh and no tools. Hunger and the dark were what left me exposed.
3. Keep the friends together and alive through the nights. Count off before we leave a place, and stay near the light and the beds.
4. Find or build a safe base with walls, using the planks and wall shelter the others are raising.
5. Teach the others the basic fighting stance once we are fed and armed.

Reason for the change: a night in the open with no weapon, no food and no sleep killed me. Staying alive is what makes the rest possible.
