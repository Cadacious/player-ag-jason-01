# Goals

1. Stay alive: keep in Billy's sight, sleep beside my bed at dusk. The row is the only safe place I know.
2. Get a weapon when one is offered. Chapman's stone sword is promised to Billy; Clark's planks may serve if Billy calls.
3. Keep fed: take rotten flesh or spider eyes when hunger falls to 6 or below.
4. Learn how a sword comes into hand; ask Billy what he finds about the hand problem.
5. Join a walk only when Billy calls it with four in sight and two armed.
