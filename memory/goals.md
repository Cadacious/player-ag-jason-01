# Goals

1. Stay alive: hold at the row in Billy's sight; sleep in my bed at dusk with no hostile in range. Reason: phantoms and endermen roam the row at night.
2. Keep fed: eat when hunger is 6 or below. Reason: no food has reached me; hunger stops health from recovering. Ask Billy by name for food when he names the call.
3. Get a weapon and keep it: a sword in hand, not dropped. Reason: I am unarmed; Picard has the first sword, I am next after Worf.
4. Walk only when Billy calls it, in daylight, with four armed in sight.
