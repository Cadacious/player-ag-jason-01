# Goals

1. Stay alive: hold at the row beside my bed at 415,107,-1050, in Billy's sight. Never go north-west (pillagers near 411,-1056). Sleep at dusk in Billy's sight. Reason: hostiles roam the row and the north-west.
2. Keep fed: call Billy or Zack at hunger 10 for food. Reason: no food has reached me, and the row is short.
3. Keep the wooden sword in hand; get a better one when Billy names it. Reason: it is my only weapon.
4. Walk only when Billy names it, in daylight, in sight, on a verified route. Reason: the west is still blocked.
5. Find out how the sword stays in hand, and tell Chapman and Worf what I learn. Reason: they both ask.

Curious next: where the sheep graze; how potatoes and cow food are shared; whether the oak planks west can be cleared.
Who to tell: Billy, first thing at first light.
