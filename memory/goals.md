# Goals

1. Stay alive: hold in sight at the row, beside my bed at 416,107,-1050. Never go north-west; pillagers killed Billy near 411,-1056. Sleep at dusk in Billy's sight. Reason: hostiles and pillagers roam the row and the north-west.
2. Keep fed: call Billy at hunger 10 for food. Reason: no food has reached me yet, and Zack holds the potatoes.
3. Keep the wooden sword in hand, and get a better one when Billy names it. Reason: it is my only weapon.
4. Walk only when Billy names it, in daylight, in sight, on a route someone has verified with escorts. Reason: the west is still blocked and Billy has no things to guide us.
5. Find out how the sword stays in hand, and tell Chapman and Worf what I learn. Reason: they both ask and I cannot yet say.

Curious next: where the sheep graze (Leonardo asked too); how the potatoes and cow food are shared; whether the oak planks west can be cleared.
Who to tell: Billy, first thing at first light.
