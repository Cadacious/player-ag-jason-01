# Goals

1. Stay alive: hold at my bed 416,107,-1050 in the row, with Billy, Worf or Zack in sight. Never go south or north-west. Sleep at dusk beside the bed. Reason: hostiles roam the row, and a pillager is in the south.
2. Walk only when Billy names it, in daylight, in sight. I cannot walk by intent (goto and follow were refused or failed on day 827), so when a walk is named, ask Billy to come to me or name a route I can see. Reason: I must not walk alone, and I have no working travel.
3. Keep fed: take a potato when Billy or Zack hands it over; call Billy or Zack at hunger 10. Reason: no food of my own yet.
4. Keep the wooden sword in hand; get a better one when Billy names it. Reason: it is my only weapon.
5. Help the weak with food when it comes: Chapman, Riker and MacDonald first, through Billy. Reason: all are near death.

Curious next: a route to the potato patch 466,107,-1066 that is not south. Who to tell: Billy, first thing at first light.
