# Goals

1. Stay alive: keep in Billy's sight, sleep in my bed at dusk.
2. Get a weapon when one is offered, or when Billy's count allows.
3. Keep fed: take rotten flesh from Chapman or spider eyes from Wesley when hunger falls to 6 or below.
4. Learn how a sword comes into hand; ask Billy or Clark.
5. Join a walk only when Billy calls it with four in sight and two armed.
