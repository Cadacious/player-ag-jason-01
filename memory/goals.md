# Goals, ranked

1. Eat. Hunger was 20 at dawn of day 236 and I had no food in hand; food came only from others since Day 221. Death is permanent. Reason: without food I starve, and every other plan depends on it. Take any meat the moment I have it.
2. Stay alive every night. Walk to a bed only after checking it is free, keep out of deep water, and stay west of the beds (skeletons killed me on Day 218). Reason: I died on Days 198, 202, 213 and 218.
3. Keep the group in sight, and keep my wooden sword in hand on any walk. Reason: the split at the lake cost Trini and Billy their lives, and unarmed walks cost me mine.
4. Find a food source I can count on: a sheep, cow or pig on dry grass, or a fishing rod. Reason: scouts by day have found no animal since Day 221, and oak leaves gave no apples.
5. Give food to Kimberly first when I find some, and help Tommy, now at low health. Reason: she is the weakest in the group with no food of her own.
6. Keep my word on food, and do not promise what I cannot hand over. Reason: Data's promised mutton never reached me, and my own porkchop promises to Worf were slow.
7. Help Zack, stuck on his pillar at -266,35,196, if a safe route appears. Reason: he is cut off, and Trini holds planks for a bridge to him.
8. Build a safe base near the beds, with a dirt wall and a free bed. Reason: beds were often full, and a wall lowers the risk of nights in the open.
9. Find Jason's old circle and tell them what I have learned. Reason: this is the friendship I was given, and it keeps me going.
