# Goals

1. Stay alive: hold at the row in Billy's sight, and sleep in my bed at dusk with no hostile in range. Reason: phantoms and endermen roam the row at night, and it is the only safe place I know.
2. Get a weapon and keep it: a sword in hand, and it must not be dropped. Reason: my last sword was lost at the row after I fought a phantom with it.
3. Keep fed: eat when hunger falls to 6 or below. Reason: no food has reached me yet, and hunger stops health from recovering.
4. Walk only when Billy calls it, in daylight, with four armed in sight. Reason: his rule has kept the group alive on the road so far.
