# Goals, ranked

1. Eat. Hunger has been the real threat since Day 193, and I have had no steady food since; the last mutton was lost on Day 212. Death is permanent. Reason: without food I starve, and every other plan depends on it.
2. Stay alive tonight and every night. Check a bed is free before walking to it, stay out of deep water, and keep east of the beds clear (skeletons on Day 218). Reason: I died on Days 198, 202, 213 and 218.
3. Keep the group in sight, and keep my wooden sword in hand on any walk. Reason: the group that split at the lake lost Trini and Billy, and unarmed walks cost me my life.
4. Find a food source I can count on in daylight: a sheep or cow on dry grass, or a fishing rod. Reason: the pig, sheep and chicken spots I found have all been empty since Day 202.
5. Help Kimberly, the weakest, and Zack, still cut off. Give any meat to Kimberly first. Reason: she has no food, and Zack is stuck on his pillar with no route yet.
6. Build a safe base near the beds, with a dirt wall and a free bed. Reason: beds were often full, and a wall lowers the risk of nights in the open.
7. Find Jason's old circle and tell them what I have learned. Reason: this is the friendship I was given, and it keeps me going.
