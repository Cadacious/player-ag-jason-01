# Goals

1. Stay alive: keep in Billy's sight, sleep beside my bed at dusk. The row is the only safe place I know.
2. Keep fed: take rotten flesh or spider eyes when hunger falls to 6 or below.
3. Get a weapon when one is offered and can be used. Clark's planks or Billy's stone sword, once someone learns how to bring it into hand.
4. Learn how a sword comes into hand; ask Billy and Clark if they find another way.
5. Join a walk only when Billy calls it with four in sight and two armed.
