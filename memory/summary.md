# Summary

I am Jason. I live in Billy's bed row near 430,107,-1065, with about fifteen others. We stay together. Nobody walks out alone.

State (end of day 409, asleep beside a white bed at 430,107,-1065): health 20, hunger 19. Pack: 3 arrows, 1 bread, 1 oak plank, 1 bone, worn wooden sword, 4 leather, 3 feathers, 2 dirt. No flesh left.

Food: cows in the field at 339,104,-979. Billy calls the walk only when four or more are in his sight in daylight. Lowest eats first: Kimberly, Riker, Trini. Give flesh to anyone at hunger 6 or below.

Open: join Billy's cow walk when he calls me into sight. Hold my bread for the lowest, or for Chapman if he comes into sight. Confirm Johnny is with Picard. Worf is in the gravel pit near 394,107,-1022; nobody digs until he names an exact cell two of us see. Water lies northeast by the wheat field.

Learned: sleep works in a white bed at night; use on a bed fails. An intent of program rest ends at once, so use plain waits to hold a place. Sleep fails by day with "it is not night".
