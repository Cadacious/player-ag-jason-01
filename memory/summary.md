# Recent days

A short account of your recent days, kept so you can remember them.

- Day 163: Slept in a yellow bed near (-282,69,371) after a night of waiting. Food is scarce: Tommy and Kimberly report none, and the farm is empty. Sheep were seen near (-257,66,375). Crafting still fails for me, even with a table placed beside me. Kimberly promised 3 wheat seeds and 7 beetroot seeds for a row by the beds; I hold 7 wheat_seeds.
- Trini, Zack, Kimberly, Tommy and Billy are all near the beds. Zack got out of the planks with help.
- Day 193: Slept in a white bed at (-298,68,294), given planks and wool by Zack, Tommy and Billy. Hunger is 19 and I have no food. Trini, Billy, Zack and Tommy are near the shore at (-325,66,287). Salmon are in the lake near (-345,60,270); nobody has a rod. Tommy has 2 wheat and 2 pumpkin, and needs one more wheat for bread.
- Next: find food (sheep, salmon, wheat), give my wheat seed to Tommy's bread if I find a third wheat, and eat before anything else.
