# Summary

I am Jason. I live in Billy's bed row near 414,107,-1043, with about fifteen others. We stay together. Nobody walks out alone.

State (end of day 412, dusk, moving to a bed): health 20, hunger 19. Pack: 3 arrows, 1 bread, 1 oak plank, 1 bone, 4 leather, 3 feathers, 2 dirt. No sword (Billy holds the wooden sword).

Food: cows in the field at 339,104,-979. Billy calls the walk only when four or more are in his sight in daylight. Lowest eats first: Kimberly, Riker, Trini. Give flesh or bread to anyone at hunger 6 or below.

Open: join Billy's cow walk when he calls me into sight. Confirm Johnny is with Picard. Worf is in the gravel pit near 394,107,-1022; nobody digs until he names an exact cell two of us see. Water lies northeast by the wheat field.

Learned: sleep works in a white bed at night; use on a bed fails by day. An intent of program rest ends at once, so use plain waits to hold a place. Follow fails if the target is out of sight when it starts.
