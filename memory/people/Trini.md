# Trini

- Impression: careful and quick to answer, and she reported skeleton ground and deep water to the west. She drowned at the sheep spot on Day 204 and is back with the group now. Days 225-236 she scouted with me and found an apple she held for Kimberly.
- Trust: 60 (unchanged). Why: good information and she kept the apple for the weakest. She walked into water and died once, and I could not hold her back. She holds about 24 planks for a bridge to Zack.
