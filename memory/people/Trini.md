# Trini

- Impression: careful and quick to answer. She reported skeleton ground and deep water to the west. She drowned at the sheep spot on Day 204 and is back with the group. Days 225-236 she scouted with me and found an apple she held for Kimberly. Days 238-248 she was the weakest of us (health 3, hunger 11 on Day 248), and I tried many times to hand her wheat and a chicken.
- Trust: 60. Why: good information, and she kept the apple for the weakest. She walked into water and died once. She has not yet eaten the wheat or chicken I tried to give her, and some of those hand-offs failed on the ground; I do not blame her for that. She holds about 24 planks for a bridge to Zack.
