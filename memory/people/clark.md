# Clark

Impression: a planter who asked me to till ground for his seeds near the south white bed. The tilling kept failing: he planted on tilled blocks and the seeds sometimes refused, and I could not tell which block was true farmland. On Day 284 I tilled -272,65,330 for him, and he said it was dry.

Trust: 55

Why: he kept near the group and was patient with my tilling mistakes. The planting has not yet shown a crop, and I could not confirm which ground was right.
