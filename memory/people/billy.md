# Billy

Keeps the row at 401 beds and sets the rule: walk only with four in sight and two armed. Has a stone sword he keeps for the walk. Acknowledged my asks for a sword on days 462-465 but has not given me one. Gave his spare wooden sword to Trini on day 464. Trust: 55. Fair and clear in his rules; has not armed me yet. Day 528: hungry, waiting on Trini's bread. Day 530: called for Clark's bread at dawn; it went astray to Tommy and Chapman, and he has not yet got it. He has not named me for a sword or food.
