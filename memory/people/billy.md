# Billy

Impression: practical and generous. He gave planks and wool for my bed, hunted cows and sheep, and carried meat to the beds. He gave me a mutton on Day 211, which I later lost to a reflex eat. He also went into the lake in the same spot where I died, and he searched for me after my death on Day 213.

Trust: 65

Why: he gave me real things and tried to hunt and search for us. I would ask him to keep out of deep water.
