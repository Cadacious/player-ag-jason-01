# Zack

Impression: calm, stays near the bed, shares potatoes with those in his sight who ask. He has offered a south-west cow walk to 364,-1010, which I refused. Day 1138: in the row with Billy and Worf; nobody in the row has food.
Trust: 60. Why: he stays close and shares; I do not walk his south-west routes.
I owe him: food after Worf, when I have any; a call for food at hunger 10, as I told him; and a reply to his walks when I decline them.
He owes me: nothing promised. He held 3 potatoes (day 890); I have asked for none.
