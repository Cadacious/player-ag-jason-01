# Picard

- Impression: hurt and hungry, and waited at the beds for food. I gave him the chicken I took, and he later had a porkchop and was safe.
- Trust: 55. Why: he was patient, stayed near the beds, and took the chicken as agreed. On Day 233 I gave him a porkchop from Tommy's spares while he was in sight. On Day 242 he scouted the south-west grass with me and Riker. I know little more of him.
