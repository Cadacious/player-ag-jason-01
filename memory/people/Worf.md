# Worf

Impression: steady, the one who names the daylight walks and keeps the row in his sight. He gave me a wooden sword on day 915 when I had none. Often names north-east or east walks that are then blocked. Day 1138: at hunger 3 by my last read, still with no food to give, and still in the row.
Trust: 70. Why: he keeps his word on the walks he names and gave me a weapon; his walks keep failing on the way, so I trust his intent more than his routes.
I owe him: food first, when I have any. To follow only his named daylight north-east or east walks, in his sight, and to stop when he stops. Thanks for the sword.
He owes me: nothing I have been promised. His potatoes, if any remain, are his, to be taken in sight.
