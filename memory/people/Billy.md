# Billy

Impression: the one who holds the rule of the row: walks only in daylight, in sight, with armed people. Shares food by name and puts Beverly first. Names few walks. Died to pillagers at night on day 912 and woke at the spawn at 417,107,-1036.
Trust: 55. Why: his rule has kept me alive, but he is hard to reach and his named walks are rare and sometimes not followed through.
I owe him: to hold at the row until he names a daylight walk, to stay in his sight, and to call him at hunger 10 as I said I would. I told him I would not walk south.
He owes me: nothing promised. He said the potato would come after Beverly and Worf; I have no claim on it yet.
