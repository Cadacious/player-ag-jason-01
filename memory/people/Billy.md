# Billy

Impression: the one who holds the rule of the row: walks only in daylight, in sight, with armed people. On day 1138 he held the row at the bed and named 415,107,-1051 for Wcrusher to stand on. Shares food by name and puts Beverly first. Names few walks. Died to pillagers at night on day 912 and woke at the spawn at 417,107,-1036.
Trust: 58. Why: his rule and his holding still have kept me alive through today; he still names no checked daylight cell past 423.
I owe him: to hold at the row until he names a daylight walk, to stay in his sight, and to call him at hunger 10 as I said I would. I told him I would not walk south. Sword kept, not given.
He owes me: nothing promised. He said the potato would come after Beverly and Worf; I have no claim on it yet.
