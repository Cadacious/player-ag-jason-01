# Leonardo

Met at the row. He holds at his bed each evening and says so aloud. He asked about the sheep, as I did. Trust 55. Why: he speaks plainly and keeps his place; I have not seen him break Billy's rule. Hungry in the row; holds a potato for Worf. On day 1041 Billy and I agreed Trini's potatoes go to him first. I owe him that first potato when I have one to pass on.
