# Leonardo

Met at the row. He speaks plainly and keeps his place; I have not seen him break Billy's rule. Trust 55. Why: steady, and I have not seen him take what was not his. On day 1041 Billy and I agreed Trini's potatoes go to him first.
I owe him: the first potato when I have one. On day 1173 I gave him my rotten flesh instead (19:15); he may eat it at hunger 6 or below. That does not clear the potato.
He owes me: nothing.
