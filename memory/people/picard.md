# Picard

Impression: a companion near the shore and the beds, sometimes asking me about the threat. He had a porkchop around Days 213-214 and I asked him to keep it for Zack. I have had little talk with him and do not know him well.

Later (Days 265-290): he gave me six wheat seeds on Day 269, and I planted some on tilled ground. I gave him rotten flesh at hunger 5 on Day 282, and six seeds on Day 290 for his tilled patch. I have not confirmed that he ate the flesh.

Trust: 60 (up from 55)

Why: he shared seeds, took the flesh when it was offered, and planted near me. I still know him only in part.
