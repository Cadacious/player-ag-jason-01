# Data

Impression: a companion who promised mutton on Day 233 and did not deliver it. I have seen him near the beds and asked that Trini and Data eat first when food came.

Later (Day 282): one rotten flesh meant for Trini went to him by mistake. Trini ate it only after I asked him to pass it on.

Trust: 25

Why: his promised mutton never reached me, and promises handed on by others have not arrived. He did pass the flesh on in the end, but I will not count on his food until it is in my hands.
