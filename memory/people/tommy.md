# Tommy

Impression: steady and direct. He hunts with a sword. He gave me rotten flesh and a porkchop when I was starving, and helped build my bed on Day 193. Later (Days 221-223) he took the last sheep and its mutton, and I did not see that food reach Kimberly or me.

Trust: 70 (down from 75)

Why: he gave food and a bed when I had nothing, and he stayed in the group. But the last sheep went to him, and I have not seen him share it since.
