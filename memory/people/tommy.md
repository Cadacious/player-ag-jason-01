# Tommy

Impression: steady and direct. He hunts with a sword. He gave me rotten flesh and a porkchop when I was starving, and helped build my bed on Day 193. Later (Days 221-223) he took the last sheep and its mutton, and I did not see that food reach Kimberly or me.

Later (Days 265-290): his two promised porkchops still had not reached me by Day 268, and I have not seen them since. He stays near the group and answers when called.

Trust: 60 (down from 70)

Why: he gave food and a bed when I had nothing, and he stayed in the group. But the last sheep went to him, and his two promised porkchops never reached my hand.
