# Tommy

Impression: steady and direct. He hunts with a sword. He gave me rotten flesh and a porkchop when I was starving, and helped build my bed on Day 193. Later (Days 221-223) he took the last sheep and its mutton, and I did not see that food reach Kimberly or me.

Later (Days 251-264): he promised two porkchops at the white beds and had not brought them by Day 264. He stays near the group and answers when called.

Trust: 70

Why: he gave food and a bed when I had nothing, and he stayed in the group. But the last sheep went to him, and his promised porkchops have not reached me.
