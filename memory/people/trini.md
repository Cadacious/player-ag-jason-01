# Trini

Impression: watchful and practical. She warned me about the iron golem and a creeper, and she sees danger early. She hunted cows and shared beef. On Days 214-223 she took my three logs for planks, holds about 24 planks, and leads the route to Zack's pillar. She also held mutton for Zack when I was waiting in the open.

Later (Days 251-264): she gave food first, including wheat to me on Day 264 when I was weakest. Her porkchop given on Day 256 never reached my pack, so I now eat food the moment I hold it.

Trust: 65 (down from 70)

Why: her warnings kept me out of danger, and she has worked the bridge plan well. She shares when she has food, but the porkchop hand-off failed. The archer incident near the cows was not her fault, but it cost meat I was counting on. I drowned in the lake while she was out of my sight, so I trust her more when she is in sight.
