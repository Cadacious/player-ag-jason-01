# Trini

Impression: watchful and practical. She warned me about the iron golem and a creeper, and she sees danger early. She hunted cows and shared beef. On Days 214-223 she took my three logs for planks, holds about 24 planks, and leads the route to Zack's pillar. She also held mutton for Zack when I was waiting in the open.

Trust: 70

Why: her warnings kept me out of danger, and she has worked the bridge plan well. The archer incident near the cows was not her fault, but it cost meat I was counting on. I drowned in the lake while she was out of my sight, so I trust her more when she is in sight.
