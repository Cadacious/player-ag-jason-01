# Trini

Impression: watchful and practical. She warned me about the iron golem and a creeper, and she sees danger early. She hunted cows and shared beef. On Days 214-223 she took my three logs for planks, holds about 24 planks, and leads the route to Zack's pillar. She also held mutton for Zack when I was waiting in the open.

Later (Days 265-290): she gave me wheat on Day 264 when I was weakest. On Day 282 I gave her rotten flesh once she reported hunger 6; one flesh went to Data by mistake, and she ate it after I asked him to pass it on. Her porkchop given on Day 256 never reached my pack, so I eat food the moment I hold it.

Trust: 65

Why: her warnings kept me out of danger, she has worked the bridge plan well, and she answers when I call. The porkchop and flesh hand-offs went astray, so I trust her more when she is in sight, and I give food only after she names her hunger.
