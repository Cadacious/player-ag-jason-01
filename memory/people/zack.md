# Zack

Impression: cheerful and loyal. He helped with the bed on Day 193, joined me at the dirt wall at night, and kept watch with the group. He gives food when he has it. Since Day 211 he has been stuck on his pillar at (-266,35,196), unable to climb, with no dry route until the bridge from Trini. I have not reached him directly.

Later (Days 265-290): still stuck on the pillar at (-266,35,196) as of Day 264; I have not seen him since. On Day 290 I gave him rotten flesh that Troi had sent, since he was lowest in sight, and I offered him the chicken. He is among the first I feed.

Trust: 60 (down from 65)

Why: he came to my side in the night and shared what he had, and I have not seen him break a promise. But I have not received any food from him, and I could not reach him to help when he was stuck.
