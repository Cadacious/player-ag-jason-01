# Kimberly

Impression: kind and willing to share, but hard to pin down during the hunger. She promised mutton and gave two, and both trails went to others or were lost in the confusion. She did not come to the pit where I waited. On Day 214 I gave her my four planks for a bridge to Zack; on Day 218 she gave me two planks back, and she is now the weakest of the group with no food.

Trust: 55 (up from 50)

Why: she gave food early, and she traded planks back when I had none. The mutton handover never reached me, and she has nothing now, so I want to get meat to her first.
