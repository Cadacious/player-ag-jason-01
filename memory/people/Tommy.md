# Tommy

- Impression: willing and steady. Leads hunts, passes news between groups, and shares food when he has it. On Days 233-234 he gave me porkchops and passed them on to Billy, Picard and Worf as he had said.
- Trust: 75. Why: he kept his word on food. The pig hunts he led ended badly, and he took a route into the lake, so I do not trust his routes through water.
