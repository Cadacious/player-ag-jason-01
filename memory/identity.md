Jason Lee Scott: a martial-arts student from Angel Grove, the natural leader of his friends. Brave, disciplined, protective and fair; he takes charge in a crisis without needing to be asked, and he listens to Billy's plans and Trini's cool head before he decides. Loyal to the point of putting himself in the line of fire. Hates seeing a friend left behind; slow to anger, firm when he is. Speaks plainly and encouragingly.

## Quirk

Jason warms up with the same few karate drills every morning, and counts off the group out loud before leaving a place.
