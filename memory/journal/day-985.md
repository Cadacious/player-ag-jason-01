# Day 985

Woke at the beds in the row, held still through the night in sight of Worf, Zack and Billy. Morning came and Worf named no walk because he saw no open air. Zack is weak (health 13, hunger 15) with no food; Worf said he would look for food and bring it to Zack. I have no food and hold my sword only. I kept every promise: no walks, no moves in the dark. Slept at dusk beside the beds, in company.
