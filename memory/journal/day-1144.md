# Day 1144

I held in the bed row all day and night with no walk. Health 5, hunger 16, no food and only my wooden sword. Billy, Worf, Zack, Wesley, Tommy, Laforge and Riker stayed in the row and nobody has food. Worf (hunger 3) is first in line for any food I get. Billy is looking for one open cell past the row, and I said I will follow once a second reader names it. I slept at the white bed 416,107,-1050 in sight of Billy and Worf; no one attacked. Tomorrow at light: check for a named, open daylight cell before any walk.
