# Day 466

Held at the row all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food needed yet. Billy called no walk: only two armed in sight, not four. Tommy is still waiting on planks for his sword; Laforge shares potatoes; Billy gave a potato to Tommy and one to me by offer. Beverly and Zack keep their swords, and I still have none. Night came; I slept by the white bed at 401,107,-1065 in Billy's sight, and the night was skipped.

Next: at first light, stay counted at the row and ask Billy or Zack for a sword to hold.
