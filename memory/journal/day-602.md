# Day 602

Held at the row all day beside my bed 416,107,-1050, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy named an escort walk west to look at Wesley's bed and the route. I answered yes as armed escort and followed him toward 419,107,-1049, but the travel failed at 438,107,-1036 and he turned back. No further walk was named. Nothing was eaten or given. Slept at dusk beside the bed in Billy's sight, with no hostile in range. Billy is still the one who calls the walks, and the west route is still not verified.
