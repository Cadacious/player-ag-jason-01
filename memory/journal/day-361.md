# Day 361

Quiet day at the bed row near 429,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. I held 2 oak planks for Worf's shelter; he was still out of sight, so they are not yet given. Bows were drawn somewhere to the south and east during the night, but nothing reached us. Billy, Picard, Troi, Leonardo, Data and I went to bed in one group. My first sleep was cut short; the second worked, and 8 of 10 needed were asleep. Johnny's safety with Picard is still unconfirmed. Next: give the planks to Worf at first light, join the cow walk in Billy's sight, check the food chest at 433,107,-1023.
