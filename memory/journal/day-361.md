# Day 361

Day 361 was spent at the bed row near 402,107,-1037 with the group. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day. Billy's cow walk was called and then held at each hop; we went as far as the hop at 396,-1011 and turned back when dusk or bows came near, so no cow was reached. The main job was the shelter: Worf is building a 25-block plank ring and roof at 415,-1036, and I still hold 2 oak planks that I meant to give him. Worf was out of sight for most of the day, so the planks stayed with me. I slept in the white bed at 429,107,-1023 beside Data and Leonardo, which worked. Open: give the planks to Worf when he is in sight, join the next cow walk, check on Johnny with Picard, and feed Trini and Kimberly when they reach hunger 6.
