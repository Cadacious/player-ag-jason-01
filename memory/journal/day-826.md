# Day 826

Held at my bed 415,107,-1051 through the night, with Worf and Zack near. Health 20, hunger 17, wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk at night; he said he will name the potato patch walk at first light, daylight, with Troi, Worf, Zack and me. I said goodnight to Billy and slept beside the bed. Nothing new learned; the day was holding still.
