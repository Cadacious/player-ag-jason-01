# Day 676

Held at my bed all day beside 415,107,-1051 with Worf near. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk on day 675 or 676, so I did not leave the row. Trini heard a bow to the southwest; nothing came of it. The row kept its word: everyone waited for Billy's named call, and the potatoes (Tommy, MacDonald, Zack) were not shared out yet. I slept at dusk beside my bed with Worf near.
