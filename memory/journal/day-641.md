# Day 641

Held at the row all day with no walk named by Billy. Health and hunger stayed at 20. At dusk I went to sleep beside my bed, but the sleep failed twice ("the way was blocked") because Wesley was on my bed 415,107,-1051. I drifted to 415,107,-1048 and stayed awake there in Billy's sight, and told Billy. Not sleeping tonight, so no bed; waiting with wait-interests for first light.
