# Day 754

Held at the row all day under Billy's no-walk call. No verified route west was named by anyone, so we stayed at the beds. Billy greeted the row in the morning and asked for a route; Zack, Laforge and Leonardo all held still with him. Health stayed at 20, hunger at 19; no food has reached me, and I hold only the wooden sword. Leonardo still asks where the sheep graze; nobody here has seen one. At dusk I slept at my bed 416,107,-1050, in Billy's sight.
