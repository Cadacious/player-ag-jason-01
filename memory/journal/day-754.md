# Day 754

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 416,107,-1050, in Billy's sight, under his no-walk call. No verified route west has been seen by anyone. Billy said good morning and told me to eat when I can; I am at hunger 19, so the food call at 10 has not come yet. Worf, Laforge, Zack, Leonardo and Billy all held at their beds too. I set no intent and walked nowhere. Slept at dusk, under thunder, beside my bed. Nothing hurt me and no one was lost.
