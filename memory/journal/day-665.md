# Day 665

Held at the row by my bed at 415,107,-1047 all day. Billy named no walk. He handed John his wooden sword and went to bed beside John's row. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Heard many row voices, mostly holding for Billy's word. Still no sheep seen; cows only at 339,104,-979 (Data). MacDonald and Troi are thin, Data is at 13. Slept at dusk at my bed row, nothing hostile near. Nothing new learned about the west route.
