# Day 704

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 416,107,-1050, in sight of Worf and Wecrusher. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy did not come or name a call, and no walk was named; the west is still blocked and no route is verified. Zack has 3 potatoes and offers them to anyone in sight; Worf is at hunger 17, Laforge and Beverly hold no food. Cow leads remain at 434,106,-1113 and 339,104,-979, with no sheep seen. At dusk I slept beside my bed in sight of the row. Nothing hurt me and nothing new was found.
