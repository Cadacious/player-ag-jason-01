# Day 685

Held at my bed 416,107,-1050 in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy was not in sight and named no walk, so I did not walk. Worf and Wesley stayed near me; the row held. Picard reported cows sighted near 339,104,-979, the only food lead, and asked Billy to name the share. MacDonald is the thinnest (health 11 earlier) and Trini is at hunger 17. Tommy holds 3 potatoes. Nothing new was learned about the west route. Slept at dusk beside my bed.
