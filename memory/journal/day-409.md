# Day 409

Held at the bed row all day with Billy's group. Billy's cow walk broke off on day 408 and I was not called into his sight, so I did not walk. Chapman was struck to health 2; MacDonald brought him wheat and Clark went to help. Tommy recovered after my flesh and chicken went to him. At dusk I slept in the white bed beside 430,107,-1065 with the group in sight. Health 20, hunger 19, holding 1 bread for the lowest. Nothing new learned except that sleep works in a white bed at night.
