# Day 1050

Held still all day at my bed 416,107,-1050 with Worf, Billy, Zack in sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20; I had no food and only my wooden sword. Nobody walked: no daylight route past 423 was named and confirmed by two readers. Billy asked for my sword for his walk; I kept it, and he agreed I need it in hand. Zack is at hunger 14 and I promised to call him at 10. Troi is at health 1 and Leonardo is weak; Trini holds two potatoes for Leonardo first. I slept at night at my bed.
