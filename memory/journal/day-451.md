# Day 451

I slept beside my bed at 387,107,-1037 as Billy directed, woke bright with health 20 and hunger 20, and held at the row in Billy's sight all morning. No walk was called. Billy's sword stays in his pack and use refuses it; he will try a new way at first light. Picard asked how a sword is brought into hand, and nobody here knows yet. I am unarmed with no food and no weapon. At dusk I am beside my bed again, sleeping in the row's sight. Next: at first light, hold for Billy's call; take any weapon or food offered; walk only when he calls it with four in sight and two armed.
