# Day 1042

I woke at my bed 416,107,-1050 with health 20 and hunger 20, pack holding only my wooden sword. I held still all day in sight of Billy, Worf, Zack and Troi. No walk was named, and no clear cell east past 423 was seen by anyone. Leonardo is at health 1 and Trini holds two potatoes for him first; I had nothing to pass on. Zack is at hunger 14 and Billy and Worf are hungry too. I said where I stood and that I walk only on a named daylight route. I slept at dusk in my bed.
