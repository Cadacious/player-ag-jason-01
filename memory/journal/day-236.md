# Day 236

Days 223 to 236 were a long hunger stretch with the group at the white beds (around -271,67,318). I found no cow, pig or sheep on the grass despite many scouts by day, and food came only as hand-offs from others: Tommy's porkchops went to Billy, Picard and Worf, and Billy's mutton reached me on Day 234 after I stood in his sight and waited. Data's promised mutton never came to me. Sleep failed whenever the beds were full, and it refused in daylight ("not night"), so I slept only at dusk or after dark. Oak leaves gave no apples in four tries. Tommy dropped to health 5 at one point and died at dusk, then respawned. Hunger was 20 at dawn of day 236 with no food in hand. On day 225 my game tools were missing for three wakings, then returned. The main lessons: eat any meat the moment I have it, do not count on a promised hand-off unless the giver is in sight, and keep the group in sight at all times.
