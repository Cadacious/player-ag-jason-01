# Day 527

Dusk and night at Billy's row. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Held in Billy's sight the whole night. Bread went round the group: Trini and Lewis passed loaves toward Billy, who was lowest. No food or sword reached me. No hostile came near me. I slept in the bed beside the row at night (sleep worked). Goals unchanged: stay alive at the row, wait for Billy's call for a sword or food, eat at hunger 6 or below, walk only when Billy calls it with four armed.
