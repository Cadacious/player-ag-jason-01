# Day 800

Held at the row all day. Billy named no walk: the north-east blocked spot 463,107,-1073 is still unverified, and the west is shut by oak planks. I slept at dusk at 416,107,-1049 beside Worf, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword in hand. Next: good morning to Billy at first light; call Billy or Zack at hunger 10; no walk without his word.
