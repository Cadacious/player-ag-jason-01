# Day 800

Held at my bed beside Worf in the row all day, in Billy's sight. No walk was named. I gave Billy the blocked spot as I know it: north-east toward 463,107,-1073; I still cannot name the exact tile. Health stayed at 20; hunger fell to 17 and no food has reached me. Billy said he is holding for a verified route, and that anyone who sees food or sheep should tell him. Sleep at dusk in the row, as the rule says.
