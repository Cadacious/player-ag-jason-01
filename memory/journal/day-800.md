# Day 800

Held at my bed 416,107,-1050 beside Worf, in Billy's sight, all day. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Hunger stayed at 17 with no food; Billy's rule is to call at hunger 10. Billy still wants the exact tile of the blocked north-east spot; my notes say 463,107,-1073 but I have not seen it. Billy and Laforge said goodnight at dusk. I slept at the row in Billy's sight. Nothing new was learned today beyond that the row holds steady.

Who I dealt with: Billy (steady, fair, holding the rule), Worf (holding the bed beside me), Laforge (holding, no food), Zack (named only at night).

Next: at first light, good morning to Billy; give him the blocked-spot note; call for food at hunger 10.
