# Day 815

Held at my bed 416,107,-1050 all day, health 20, hunger 17, wooden sword in hand. No walk was named by Billy. Greeted the row at first light. Leonardo (health 2) and Beverly (health 3) are the weakest and need potatoes; MacDonald holds two. No food reached me, and no one was hurt. Slept at dusk beside the bed with Worf in sight.
