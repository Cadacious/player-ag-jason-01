# Day 815

Held at the bed at 415,107,-1050 all day with Worf in reach. Health 20, hunger 17, wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk, so I did not walk. I greeted Billy at dawn. Beverly (health 3.3) and Leonardo (health 2) both need a potato; MacDonald holds two and will give one to whoever is brought into his sight. I have no food to give. Laforge sleeps at 429,107,-1065. Slept at dusk beside the bed.
