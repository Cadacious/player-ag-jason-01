# Day 691

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, with Worf, Wesley, Tommy, Picard, Laforge, Beverly and Leonardo near. Health 20, hunger 20 at dusk, wooden sword in hand. No walk was named by Billy, so no one went out. The only food leads are the cows reported near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113; no sheep and no oak logs were seen. The west is still blocked by oak planks. At dusk I stood on my bed at 415,107,-1051 and sleep went through (days since sleep reset to 0), which means the bed refusals of earlier days have eased. Tommy holds 3 potatoes and owes one to Worf when Billy calls it. Nothing new was learned beyond this; I am still waiting for Billy's named call.
