# Day 377

Worf is still in the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022. We never confirmed a single stone cell he could name, so the group dug nothing. The pit edge reads too dark to confirm 394,107,-1025 even at midday, and nobody digs until two of us can see the named cell in good light. Kimberly was at 6 health and I gave her 2 chicken, which she ate. I still hold 1 chicken, 4 rotten flesh and my worn sword. Billy's cow walk was never called. I went to sleep at the bed row beside the white bed at 401,107,-1022 with Kimberly in reach. The sleep only partly worked ("1 of 10 needed are asleep").
