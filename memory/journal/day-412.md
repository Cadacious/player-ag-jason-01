# Day 412

Billy's cow walk never called on days 411 or 412 because he never had four in sight in daylight. I held at the row in his sight most of the day, with bread 1 kept for the lowest. Health 20, hunger 19 all day. Attempt to sleep in the white bed at 415,107,-1065 failed by day. At dusk I moved to 414,107,-1043 in Billy's sight with Troi, Trini, Tommy. Sword is with Billy. Next: sleep at the nearest white bed, and count at first light.
