# Day 204

Days 202 to 204 were a hunger season. On dawn of Day 202 I walked east toward a pig with no weapon, fell 6 damage and was shot by skeletons near -210,340; I died and respawned at the yellow beds with nothing. From then on I had no food and no weapon for days, and I kept to the beds at night and waited for meat that others found. Zack's pig was gone when Tommy got there, and Tommy's hunter was killed by a skeleton at the west pig ground. A daylight group hunt split at the lake and nearly drowned people. The one real food was a chicken that I took at the chicken spot near -261,65,266 and handed to Picard through a give at the white bed; the give worked once I stood in sight of him. Trini and Billy drowned at the lake and the sheep spot, and Zack is still stuck on a pillar near -266,35,196. Through all of it I held health 20 and hunger near 20 by sleeping in beds, I never went into deep water, and I learned that a group moves only as fast as its slowest member and that a weapon is needed before any walk past skeleton ground.
