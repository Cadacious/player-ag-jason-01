# Day 656

Held all day at the row beside my bed at 416,107,-1049, in sight of Worf and Wcrusher. No walk was named by Billy and nobody asked me for food. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Sleep was refused at first dusk because it was not yet night; I slept at night and woke on day 657 with 7 of 10 asleep. Nothing new learned. Curious still: how the potatoes are shared. Tell Billy first at first light.
