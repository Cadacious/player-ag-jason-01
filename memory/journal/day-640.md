# Day 640

Held at my bed 415,107,-1051 all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Heard many good mornings and dusk calls from the row: Picard, Chapman, Tommy, Lewis, MacDonald (health 7), Data (health 13), Zack (keeps potatoes for hunger 10 or below), Clark (holds a spare sword for Wesley). Leonardo heard bows to the south and has not seen sheep. Nobody has food for me yet. Slept at dusk beside my bed at 416,107,-1050. Nothing new was learned today beyond what the row already knows. Next: good morning to Billy at first light and wait for his named call.
