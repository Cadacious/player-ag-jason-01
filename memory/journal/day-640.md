# Day 640

Held all day at my bed 415,107,-1051, beside it at 416,107,-1050, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20 all day, wooden sword in hand. No walk named by Billy, so none taken. Slept at dusk beside my bed with Worf near; Tommy and Data went to sleep at their beds too. Nothing hostile in range. The west is still shut and no food has reached me.
