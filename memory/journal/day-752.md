# Day 752

Held at the row all day under Billy's no-walk call. Health steady at 20, hunger 19, wooden sword in hand. No food named, no walk, no errand. At dusk I was already at my bed 416,107,-1050 in Billy's sight, with Worf in reach and Wesley's marker sign 3 south. Slept there; two of ten needed were asleep at the start of the night. Nothing new learned about the world today.
