# Journal
- Day 1145, light. Held at the white bed 415,107,-1050 with the wooden sword. Night sleep failed (way blocked); a dark-shelter reflex took cover. Pillagers reported west and north-east. Told Tommy to come into my sight. No food, hunger 15, health 5. No walk.
- Day 1146, light. Woke at the row. Health 5, hunger 15, no food. Zack, Riker, Trini and Wesley all hold; nobody has food, nobody walks. Said we hold until two armed readers name a checked open cell. No walk. Waiting.
