# Journal
- Day 1147 night: held at bed with sword, health 5, hunger 15. Riker asked for a daylight route; I have no checked cell, so I asked Billy and Worf to name one. No walk.
- Day 1148 morning: Billy says the row is enclosed in oak, no open cell; Worf (hunger 3) and Trini name no route. I hold with the sword, no food, health 5.
- Day 1148 dusk: held at my bed with Worf, Zack, Billy near. Sleep started, then daylight came; still awake at the bed.
- Day 1148 daylight: said I hold with the sword and ask Billy and Worf for a checked open cell. Zack, Trini, Laforge, Riker all hold too. No food, no route, no walk. Health 5, hunger 15.
