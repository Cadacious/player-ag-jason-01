# Day 724

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 416,107,-1050 with Worf near, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named no walk and no food; he said good morning and told us to hold at the beds until someone shows a verified route. Wesley asked for a bed, was sent to 443,107,-1037, then to 429,107,-1036, and was blocked on the way. Zack offered potatoes to anyone who steps into his sight; Trini and Worf are at hunger 16 to 17 and waiting. Nothing new was found, no walk was made, and I slept at dusk beside my bed, in Billy's sight.
