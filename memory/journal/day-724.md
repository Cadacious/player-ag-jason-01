# Day 724

Held at my spot 416,107,-1050 with my wooden sword in hand all evening. Health 20, hunger 20, never hurt. Billy, Zack and I said goodnight at the row; Billy named no walk and no food, as before. The west stays shut by oak planks. I slept beside my bed with Worf near. Nothing new learned today beyond the row's routine. Billy's bed is 429,107,-1036; Zack's is 416,107,-1037.
