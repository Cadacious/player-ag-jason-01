# Day 716

Held at 419,108,-1050 through the day with my wooden sword in hand. Said good morning. Trini, Beverly, Worf, Laforge and Wesley all held at their beds waiting for Billy's named walk. Billy was not in my sight, and no walk or food was named. At dusk I walked to my bed square 416,107,-1050, slept there, and wrote the handoff. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout.
