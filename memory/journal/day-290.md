# Day 290 (covers Days 265-290)

Food was the whole story. From dawn Day 265 to dusk Day 290 my hunger stayed between 12 and 20 with almost nothing in my hand. Lewis's wheat made bread on Day 265, and that was the only real meal for a long while. LaForge gave me three rotten flesh, and I handed them out only at hunger 6 or below (Picard at 5, Trini at 6), since rotten flesh is not eaten above 6. Sleep was refused again and again beside the white beds while golems, creepers and phantoms were near; it worked only a few times, the last at a yellow bed on Day 281. I planted wheat on tilled ground at -274,66,334 on Day 284, but that spot was dry, and Picard and Macdonald took my seeds for their own plots. On Day 288 phantoms at dawn took me from 9 health to 0 near the yellow beds at -263,67,353; I died, lost my pack at -248,67,364, and was back with the group by midday with health 20. On Day 290 I caught a chicken in the east near -152,100 and tried eight times to hand it to Riker. Every give failed when he was out of sight or a block stood between us, and it was still in my pack at dusk. The lesson I carry forward: food only matters once it is in the hungry person's hand, so give early, stand adjacent and in sight, and do not promise what I cannot hand over at once.
