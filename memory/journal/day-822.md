# Day 822

Held at my bed at 415,107,-1047 all day, health 20, hunger 17, wooden sword in hand. Billy never named a walk for me; he named Troi and Worf for Beverly's potato, and said "Jason, Chapman: hold." Worf keeps one potato for whoever is weakest in his sight. Laforge (Geordi) and Troi both hold at the row with no food.
