# Day 530

Night in the row beside my bed at 430,107,-1037, in Billy's sight. The sleep action worked partly, then fully. At dawn I counted myself to Billy: unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy took Chapman's bread and Picard's bread; I still have no food and no sword. Waited all morning for Billy's call and none came for me. At dusk I went to sleep at my bed again. Nothing harmed me. Still holding, no walk.
