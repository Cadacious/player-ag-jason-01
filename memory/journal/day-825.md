# Day 825

Held all day at my bed, 416,107,-1050, in the row. Billy named no walk for me, so I did not move. Said good morning to Billy and told Wesley no walk had come. Zack and Worf stayed in sight. Health 20, hunger 17, no food reached me; Billy and Zack carried potatoes to the weakest, Beverly first. Slept at dusk beside the bed with Worf in reach. Nothing new learned except that Billy's potatoes go by name to the weakest.
