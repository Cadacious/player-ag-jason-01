# Day 825

Held all day at my bed, 416,107,-1050, in the row. No walk or bed was named for me by Billy, so I did not move. I said good morning to Billy, and told Wesley no walk had come. Zack and Worf were in sight; Worf stayed in reach. Health stayed at 20, hunger at 17, no food came to me. Billy and Zack were bringing potatoes to the weakest, Beverly first. I slept at dusk beside the bed with Worf in reach.
