# Day 366

Slept by the white bed at 416,107,-1023 at night, health 20, hunger 20. Billy never called the cow walk; he counted only Troi for most of the morning, and the group kept waiting in sight at the bed row. Worf is still alone in the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022 and has not come out. Trini (hunger 16) and Kimberly (hunger 17) are not yet at the feed line of 6; Beverly, Wesley, Data and Troi hold food for them. Heard bows drawn to the southeast and east several times; stayed near the group. Nothing was taken, nothing was lost.
