# Day 828

Held at my bed 416,107,-1050 all day with Worf in sight. Billy is alive and spoke at first light; I said good morning and told him I hold until he names a walk I can take. Potatoes at 409,-1054 lie north-west, so I declined them and asked him to bring food to my bed. Troi was chased by a pillager in the west near 419,-1039; Billy sent her east toward the chest. I said I would hold and keep east of the pillager. At night I slept beside my bed; the first call at dusk failed as "not night", the second at night worked. Health 20, hunger 17, wooden sword in hand.

Lesson: sleep at dusk fails; wait for full night, then call sleep.
