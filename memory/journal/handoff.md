# Handoff, day 912 dusk

Going to bed at 416,107,-1050. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Zack in reach. Worf out of my sight since his south-west cow walk; I declined it.
Pillagers are out east and west. Lewis died to arrows at 393,-1047. Macdonald is shot down. Billy and Wesley hold in the row.
Rules: no walk south or north-west; no walk in the dark; walk only on a daylight leg Worf (armed) names with armed people in sight. Sleep once per dusk from bed 416,107,-1050; if blocked, hold, no retry. Call Billy or Zack at hunger 10. Food order: Billy, Leonardo, Picard.
