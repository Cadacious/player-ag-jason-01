# Handoff, day 853 dusk

Going to sleep beside my white bed at 415,107,-1051, in the row, sheltered, not walking. Worf and Zack in reach. Health 20, hunger 15, wooden sword in hand, one potato kept for hunger 10 or the weakest.
No errand out. Billy has named no walk. Beverly holds 10 potatoes for Leonardo and Trini, until hunger 6. Riker, Troi and Laforge hold at their beds.
Next at waking: good morning to the row; hold at the bed; call Billy or Zack at hunger 10; do not go south or north-west. Sleep is refused in daylight; sleep only at night from beside the bed.
