# Handoff, day 711 (night)

Slept at night beside my bed, at 415,107,-1050, in sight of Worf and Wesley. Sleep worked. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. No errand running, no walk named by Billy. Billy not in sight tonight.

Next: at first light say good morning, wait for Billy's named call. Walk only in daylight on a verified route. Ask for food by name at hunger 6 or below. Cows known at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. Wesley's bed square 416,107,-1050 is blocked for him; he holds at 418,107,-1047.
