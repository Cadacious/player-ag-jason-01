# Handoff, day 685 (night, asleep)

At 416,107,-1050 beside my bed in the row, Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. No errand running. Billy named no walk on days 684 or 685. Worf and Wesley near me; the row is holding.

Next: at first light say good morning to Billy and wait for his named call. Walk only when he names it, in daylight, in his sight, on a verified route. Ask for food by name at hunger 6 or below.

Open for Billy: Picard reports cows sighted near 339,104,-979 and asks Billy to name the share; MacDonald is thinnest (health 11 in the earlier count). Trini is at hunger 17 and asks about the potato share from Zack.
