# Day 818

I held at my bed 416,107,-1050 with Worf all day, hunger 17, health 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy named walks to Beverly and then to Worf, and never named one for me, so I stayed put. I reported that the bow was quiet on my side when Billy asked. Leonardo was fed by Billy, Zack and Troi. Billy's potatoes went to Beverly and Worf first. I said I would call Billy at hunger 10. At dusk I slept beside the bed with Worf in reach.
