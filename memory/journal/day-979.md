# Day 979

Day stayed dim and held at the row beside the white beds. No food found anywhere in the row. Worf, Zack, Billy, Riker, Troi and Laforge all hold still, with no daylight route named, and I refuse north-west walks. Health and hunger stayed at 20. At night I went to bed at 415,107,-1051 with Worf and Zack in reach, and nine of ten needed were asleep. Leonardo and Picard are still the weakest, and nobody has food for them yet.
