# Day 673

Held at the row all day at my bed 416,107,-1050, in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk on day 672 or 673, so I did not move. Health 20, hunger 20 the whole day, wooden sword in hand. Several of the row (Tommy, MacDonald, Data, Troi, Trini) are hungry and holding; Zack and Tommy hold potatoes. Slept at dusk beside my bed with Worf near. Nothing new learned about the world today beyond the names and holdings of the row.
