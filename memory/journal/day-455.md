# Day 455

Stayed at the bed row all day, unarmed, counted, health 20, hunger 20. Clark gave oak planks to Picard, who made a wooden sword. Billy did not call a walk. Lewis asked about water and Billy's sword; I told him I had found neither. My bed attempts at dusk failed with "that block is a bed" on use, but a plain sleep worked (8 of 10 asleep) and I slept through the night. Still no way found for a sword to come into my hand. Chapman holds 10 flesh for Kimberly; I have taken no food yet.
