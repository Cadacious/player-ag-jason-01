# Day 663

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named. Billy handed Beverly a wooden sword and is making one for John; Clark's spare sword is promised to Wesley once he is in sight in daylight. Zack gave Tommy a potato. No food reached me; my hunger stayed at 20. I slept at dusk beside my bed at 416,107,-1050 with Worf near and no hostile in range, and woke healthy. Sheep and oak logs are still unseen.
