# Day 663

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk named by Billy, no food called. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Said good morning to Billy at first light. Slept at dusk beside bed 415,107,-1051 at 416,107,-1050; first sleep only partly took (5 of 10 asleep), second try returned with no change. Worf near all night. Row reports: potatoes held by Zack, Clark, Tommy and MacDonald; no sheep seen.
