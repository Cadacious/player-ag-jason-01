# Day 1133

Stayed at my white bed in the row at 416,107,-1050 all day and into night. Health 5, hunger 16, no food. Billy, Zack, Worf and Wcrusher stayed near. No one walked; no cell past 423 has been checked by two readers. I said aloud that we hold until light and then check a cell together. Slept at the bed after dark. Owed food still: Worf first, then Zack, Leonardo, Billy, Picard.
