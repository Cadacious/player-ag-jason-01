# Day 763

Held at the row all day at my bed 416,107,-1050, in Worf's sight. No walk named by Billy; no verified route west and no sheep seen by anyone. Health 20, hunger 19, no food, wooden sword in hand. A short walk back to my bed was named earlier and done safely. Billy said good night and I slept beside my bed at dusk. Nothing new learned about food or sheep today.
