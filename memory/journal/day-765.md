# Day 765

Held at my bed 416,107,-1050 all day under Billy's no-walk call. Billy died to pillagers north-west near 411,-1056 and his things lie there; nobody walks north-west. Chapman and Worf both asked how my sword stays in hand; I honestly do not know, it was there when I woke and I kept it. No sheep or verified route west seen by anyone. Health 20, hunger 19, no food; Zack holds potatoes for hunger 10. Slept beside my bed at dusk, in Billy's sight.
