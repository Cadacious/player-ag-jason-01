# Day 764

Held at the row all day under Billy's no-walk call, at my bed 416,107,-1050. Health 20, hunger 19, no food, wooden sword in hand. Billy asked again for a verified west route or sheep; I have seen neither. I greeted him, answered Chapman's question about the sword (I do not know how it got into my hand; it was there when I woke), and held still. Billy named the beds at dusk and I slept beside mine, in sight of the row, and the sleep worked.
