# Day 699

Held at the row all day beside bed 415,107,-1051, at 416,107,-1050, in sight of Worf and Wecrusher. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy's call never came; no walk was named and no food is known. Lewis, Picard, Beverly and Laforge also held at the row and said so. Went to sleep at dusk in sight of the row; 8 of 10 needed were asleep. Nothing new learned, and nothing was tried. Next: first light, good morning to Billy, and wait for his named call.
