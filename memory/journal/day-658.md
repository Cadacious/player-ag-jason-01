# Day 658

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 416,107,-1050, in Worf's sight. Billy named no walk and no food was called; hunger stayed at 20 and health at 20. Clark handed the spare wooden sword to Wesley as Billy approved. No sheep have been seen; Wesley's cows at 339,104,-979 are the only lead. Slept at dusk beside my bed with no hostile in range.
