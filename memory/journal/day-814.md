# Day 814

Held at the row all day at my bed 416,107,-1050, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 17. Worf spoke that I was near; I answered him. No walk was named, and none was taken. Laforge, MacDonald and Beverly reported holding, and Leonardo is at health 2 waiting for a potato. Pillagers struck the south of the row again in the day. At dusk I slept beside the bed with Worf and Wesley in sight, and the sleep worked. Nothing new was learned about the sword or sheep, and the oak planks west are still in place.
