# Day 1130

Held at the white bed in the row all day, with Worf, Zack, Billy, Trini, Riker, Laforge and Wesley in sight. No walk. No cell past 423 has been checked and named by two readers. I have no food. Worf is at hunger 4 and most urgent; I could give him nothing. Trini's chest at 433,107,-1051 had no potatoes. Sleep failed three times in the night, then worked at dusk. Health steady at 5, hunger 16.
