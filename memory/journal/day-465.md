# Day 465

Held at the row by my bed 401,107,-1065 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. At first light Billy's count was two armed (Trini, Zack). Beverly had a spare wooden sword but Tommy asked first for it, so I did not take it. No walk was called. At dusk Billy said no walk tonight, and I slept beside my bed in his sight. Nothing new learned except that the sword is still not mine.
