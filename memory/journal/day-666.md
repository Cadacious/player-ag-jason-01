# Day 666

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. Billy spoke first at morning and named no walk; I said good morning and waited. Sleep at dusk failed on day 665 because it was not yet night, then worked at the bed 416,107,-1050 when night came (9 of 10 asleep). Health 20, hunger 20 throughout, wooden sword in hand. Heard the row: MacDonald and Data thin, Zack and Lewis hold potatoes, Wesley's cows at 339,104,-979 still the only food lead. No new walk, no food, no danger.
