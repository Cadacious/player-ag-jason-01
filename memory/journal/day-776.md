# Day 776

Held at the row all day with no walk named by Billy. No sheep seen by anyone, no verified route west, no pillager news. Health stayed 20, hunger drifted to 18, and I still have no food. Riker fell to health 6 and Lewis has the potatoes; Zack and Beverly have none. I told Worf and Billy that I have seen no sheep. At dusk I slept in my bed at 415,107,-1050, in Billy's sight, as my goal says.
