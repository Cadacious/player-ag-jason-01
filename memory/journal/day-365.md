# Day 365

Waited at the beds for Billy's cow walk all morning. Billy could only see a few of us at a time, so he never called the walk; he could not count four in his sight. Worf is still stuck at the bottom of the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022, alone, with no food; Kimberly and Beverly tried to talk him out, and he is waiting for someone to say the block to mine. Night came without a walk. I slept at the white bed at 415,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 20.
