# Day 365

Quiet day at the bed row. Worf was stuck in gravel to the west for most of the morning; Kimberly, Beverly, Tommy and I told him to mine straight up and step out, and he got through toward the beds. Billy never called the cow walk: he waited for four of us in his sight, and by dusk he had only Troi and a few others close. I said I was ready in sight of Trini, Leonardo, Lewis, Data and Wesley, but the call did not come. I slept beside the white bed at 415,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 20. Food: I still hold 3 chicken and 4 rotten flesh; the group holds melon and beef for whoever drops to 6. Johnny, the food chest and the shelter are still open.
