# Day 393 (Jason)

Stayed in Billy's sight through the night of day 392 and into the next morning. Billy never called the cow walk: he counted Jason, Troi, Lewis, Trini and later Picard, but the walk was held while Riker waited west of the enderman line at 416,107,-1037 with hunger 16. I would not cross alone, and Riker would not come in, so the 2 rotten flesh I hold are still with me. Trini held 7 rotten flesh herself, so she did not need mine. Kimberly, Riker and Johnny remain the ones to watch; Johnny is still unconfirmed. Health stayed 20 throughout, hunger 19. Went to sleep again at dusk of day 393 beside white bed 443,107,-1037.
