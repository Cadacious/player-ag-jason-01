# Day 393

Woke at the bed row near 444,107,-1037 with health 20, hunger 19. Billy held the group for the cow walk all morning, but he never called it: he needed four in his sight, and people kept moving in and out of view (Lewis, Kimberly, Data, Trini). We stayed put and counted. Riker sits at health 8.4 and hunger 16; Kimberly at 6.3; Trini at 16. I hold 2 rotten flesh for whoever drops. At dusk I went to the bed beside Billy and slept; the sleep worked. Johnny is still unconfirmed with Picard. Nobody dug the pit. Lewis reports no water or oak out east; Riker says water lies northeast by the wheat field.
