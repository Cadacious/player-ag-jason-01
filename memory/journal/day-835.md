# Day 835

Held at my bed all day, 416,107,-1050, with Worf, Zack and Wesley in sight. Health 20, hunger 16, wooden sword in hand, one potato kept for the weakest (Leonardo first). Billy named no walk and brought no food, so I did not leave the row. Good morning said to Billy twice. Went to sleep at dusk beside my bed; the result came too late to read here.
