# Day 659

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, sword in hand. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. No walk was named and no food was called. Billy did not speak to me directly; the row greeted each morning and gave the same hold. Went to bed at dusk two blocks from my bed at 416,107,-1050, with no hostile in range. Nothing new learned about the world today, except that Tommy and MacDonald are low on food and that the row is still waiting on Billy's call.
