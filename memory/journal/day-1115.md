# Day 1115

Held at my bed 416,107,-1050 all day in the row. Health 5, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword in hand. Worf, Zack, Billy, Trini, Riker and Wesley held with me. No one named an open cell past 423 with two readers, so no walk. I said I hold and that I have no food. Slept at the bed at dusk after writing the handoff.
