# Day 330

Morning at the bed row with the group. Johnny still missing. I proposed a daylight search with company and Tommy agreed. We walked to the cow field at 364,110,-1010; Trini was there and a bow and an enderman had been seen nearby. No Johnny found. Dusk came while we were out, so I travelled back to the bed row at 401,107,-1037 and slept in the bed. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. Kimberly reported health 8 but fine on melon, so the melon stays with the weakest. Lesson: the cow field is a risky place to search late, and the return takes most of an afternoon.
