Day 329. Woke at dawn after a night of sleep by the bed row at 401,-1037 with the group. Kimberly (health 8) was the weakest; I kept my melon slice for her and offered it, but she said she was fine. Johnny is still missing. Trini and I went to the cows at 364,-1010 and found none; only a chicken far west. We came back to the bed row at dusk with the wooden sword, and slept there with company.
