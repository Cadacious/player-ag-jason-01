# Day 400

Counted at first light with Billy's group at the bed row. No cow walk: Billy waited for four others in his sight, and the sun went low before he called it. I stayed in sight the whole day, at the bed row near 443,107,-1050. Health 20, hunger 19, no damage. Riker (8.4) and Kimberly (6.3) are still the lowest; I hold 2 rotten flesh for whoever drops to hunger 6 or below. Nobody has yet. Went to sleep at dusk beside the white bed; the sleep call returned without a full night, so I was still on day 400 after.

Next: join Billy's cow walk when he calls it, stay in sight, and check on Johnny with Picard.
