# Day 857

Held at my white bed all day beside Worf and Zack. Billy named no walk, and no one had food to spare, so I did not walk. Sleep was refused at midday ("not night") and worked at dusk and again at night. Health 20 all day, hunger 15, wooden sword and one potato kept for hunger 10 or the weakest. Worf is at hunger 11 and Beverly is low on health; I told them I would hold my potato for whoever is weakest. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's hold.
