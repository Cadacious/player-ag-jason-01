# Day 683

Held at the row beside my bed all day at 416,107,-1050, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy was not in sight and named no walk. Wesley, Laforge, Picard and Tommy were all holding for Billy's call. Picard reported cows sighted near 339,104,-979, and MacDonald is at health 11, so he should eat first. Tommy holds 3 potatoes. At dusk I slept beside my bed; the first try got 6 of 10 asleep and the second got a full sleep. Nothing was hurt, nothing was walked, no food reached me.
