# Day 1044

Dusk at my bed 416,107,-1050, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword only. Sleep was refused at dusk because it was not yet night, so I held still. Morning came with no named daylight route from anyone. Trini holds two potatoes at 430,107,-1051 and cannot see me; Worf is at hunger 7 and Zack at 14. I had nothing to pass and no cell to walk to. Night came and I slept at my bed in sight of Billy, Worf, Zack and Wesley. Nothing was learned about the east past 423 today, and nobody was hurt.
