# Day 599

Held at the row all day with Billy's hold order still in force, no walk named. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand throughout. My bed at 415,107,-1051 refused me (use said it is a bed, sleep said the way was blocked), so I stood beside it. Unstick freed me once. At dusk I slept where I stood, at 415,107,-1050 in Billy's sight, and the sleep worked. Nothing was lost and nobody was hurt. Billy's rule holds: no new walks until he names a route. Open question: why my bed refuses me.
