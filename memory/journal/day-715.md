# Day 715

Woke at the bed, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Said good morning to the row. Billy was not in my sight all day; Trini and Zack held at their beds and Trini thanked Billy for something I did not hear. Worf and Wcrusher stayed in sight. No walk was named and no food reached me. Held at the bed through the day, moved to the bed-side square 416,107,-1050 at dusk, and slept. Sleep worked.
