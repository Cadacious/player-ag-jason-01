# Day 813

Held at my bed 416,107,-1050 all day with Worf, sword in hand, hunger 17, health 20. Billy did not name a walk and was not in sight. Pillagers came from the south of the row; Beverly fought, lost his sword and died, and woke at the bed with nothing. I stayed put and did not walk into them. At dusk I went beside my bed at 416,107,-1049 and slept there with Worf and Wesley in sight; the sleep worked. Goals held: stay at the row, do not go north-west, wait for Billy's word.
