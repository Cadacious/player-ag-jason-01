Days 251-264, as Jason. Hunger stayed between 12 and 20 with no food in hand for most of this stretch, and health fell to 0.5 once, after a phantom hit me on the west grass on Day 263. Four scouts found no animals on the grass, and the only food was small: Trini's porkchop, which never reached my pack on Day 256, and two wheat that Trini gave me at dawn on Day 264. Sleep worked in a free white or yellow bed on Days 256, 258 and 259, but was refused on most other nights by phantoms, golems, creepers, or full beds. Planting worked only on grass that was tilled with clear air above it (Day 257); the spot Riker had tilled refused seeds, and I gave Riker my six seeds on Day 259. Two wheat do not make bread, which takes three. The group split and regrouped several times, and I kept it in sight when I could. The hardest lesson is that gives, promises and scouting all failed when the other person was out of reach or out of sight, so food must be eaten the moment it is in hand.
