# Day 649

Held all day at the row beside my bed 415,107,-1051, in Billy's sight. Said good morning. Billy named no walk and no food reached me. Many at the row (Zack, Trini, Lewis, Laforge, Tommy, Beverly, Data, Troi, Kimberly, Leonardo, MacDonald) are holding the same way and some are hungry; MacDonald ate a potato at health 9. Health 20, hunger 20 at dusk. Went to sleep beside the bed; nothing hostile near.
