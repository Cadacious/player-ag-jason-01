# Day 649

Held all day at the row beside my bed 416,107,-1050, in Billy's sight. Said good morning; no walk was named and no food reached me. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Nothing hostile came near. Many of the row said good morning and held still for Billy's word. MacDonald is at health 9 with some potatoes; Troi and Tommy offered to share. At dusk I slept beside my bed with no one hostile in range. Nothing new learned about the west route.
