# Day 751

Held at the row all day at my bed 416,107,-1050, in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 19, wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk and no food; the west is still shut by the oak planks and the drop past 385,107, and no sheep have been seen. Worf drifted near my bed once and I asked him to use the other bed. At dusk Billy said good night and I slept there; sleep worked. Nothing new to act on, so I wait for first light and Billy's call.
