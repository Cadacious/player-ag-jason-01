# Day 346

Day 345 and 346 were spent mostly at the bed row. On day 345 I gave Trini the oak plank and my spare sword, then walked to the cow field with Billy's group. No cows were there, and a skeleton was near. On the morning of day 346 Billy called a cow walk to 339,-979, but his route went through a pit near 364,-1000 and was blocked. I stayed at the beds with Wesley and Tommy and did not walk alone. I slept at the bed at 429,107,-1037 and held 4 beef and 3 chicken for the lowest. Health 17.6, hunger 14. The food chest at 433,107,-1051 was empty on day 344.
