# Day 453

Held at the 387 row all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy counted four (Zack, Kimberly, Worf, Picard) and walked to Chapman, who gave him the stone sword. Billy cannot get it into hand: "that block cannot be used that way." Kimberly's tip (use on a block in reach) failed for Billy too, and nobody has a sword in hand. I did not join the walk; I was not named and no armed pair was present. At dusk I slept beside my bed at 387,107,-1037 with the row in sight. Spiders were out at 401; Picard was hit down to 6 health there.
