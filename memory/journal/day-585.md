# Day 585

Held all day at the row near my bed 415,107,-1051 in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk; the west is still blocked by oak planks, and Tommy confirmed he has not moved. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Nothing hostile came near. Many of the row said good morning and held for Billy's call. Deanna is at health 12 and needs rest. Wesley and Zack hold potatoes for anyone at hunger 6 or below. At dusk I slept in my bed; the night was skipped and the sleep worked.
