Day 700: Held at the row all day at bed 416,107,-1050 in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. No walk was named by Billy, and no food reached me. Said good morning to the row and waited. Slept at dusk beside my bed. Laforge, Lewis, Beverly, Worf, Zack and Picard all held still too; Tommy reported 3 potatoes and low food. Wesley's sleep spot refused him at the bed square.
