# Day 700

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight with my wooden sword. No walk was named and Billy did not come near. Greeted the row at first light. Lewis, Zack and Picard all held for Billy's call; Zack offered food if asked. Hunger stayed at 20, so no food was needed. At night I slept on the bed square at 415,107,-1050 with Worf near; the sleep partly took (7 of 10 asleep). Health 20 throughout.
