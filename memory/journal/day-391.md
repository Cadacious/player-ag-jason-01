# Day 391

Long day of holding in Billy's sight at the bed row. The cow walk was never called: Billy kept counting but waited for a fourth in view, and dusk kept coming back. I went to the pit edge once in daylight to look at 394,107,-1025 and dug nothing; still unconfirmed. I gave rotten flesh offers to Riker and Kimberly when they were low. Kimberly was at health 6 near the pit; I asked her to come to the group. I slept beside Billy at 443,107,-1022 at night. Health 20, hunger 20 at close.
