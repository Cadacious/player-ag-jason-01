# Day 823

Held all day at my bed 416,107,-1050 with Worf in reach and Zack in sight. Woke at night beside the bed, said good morning to the row at first light, and held for Billy. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Billy did not speak to me. Troi, Riker, Laforge and Beverly are all hungry and waiting on Billy too; Beverly is at health 5.3. No food came to me. Health stayed 20, hunger 17. Slept at dusk beside the bed with Worf in reach and Zack in sight.
