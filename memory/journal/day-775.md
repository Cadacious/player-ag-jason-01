# Day 775

Held at the row all day beside Worf under Billy's no-walk call. No food reached anyone; hunger fell to 18. Heard Zack, Chapman, Leonardo, Laforge, Picard and Wesley all asking for sheep sightings and for a named walk. Nobody has seen sheep. At night I moved to my bed at 416,107,-1050, tried to sleep in Billy's sight, and it took after a second try. Slept. Health 20 at waking.
