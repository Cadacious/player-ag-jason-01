# Day 643

Held all day beside my bed at 416,107,-1050, in Billy's sight, sword in hand. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not walk. Health and hunger stayed at 20. At dusk I slept beside my bed with no hostile in range; the sleep worked. The row was calm, with many neighbours saying good night and good morning. Potatoes are held by Clark, Zack and MacDonald; I still have none. Nothing new was learned about the west route.
