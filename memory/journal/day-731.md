# Day 731

Held at my bed in the row all day, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Greeted Billy at first light. Billy named no walk and no food, and the route west is still unverified, so nobody moved. Trini, Worf, Zack and Laforge all held too. Nobody reached hunger 10 and nobody called for food. Wesley stood in Billy's sight near 418,107,-1047. At dusk Billy went to bed at 429,107,-1037 with no walk for the night. I slept beside my bed at 416,107,-1050.
