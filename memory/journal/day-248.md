# Day 248

Days 236 to 248 were a long hunger. No food came from the grass: the scouts for sheep, cows and pigs found no animal, the salmon in the deep lake were out of reach, and oak leaves gave no apples. Wheat from one field went first to Trini, then to Riker, but each hand-over that landed on the ground failed, and the chicken Riker held went to Trini only after many failed gives; for a while I held it myself and ate it when my hunger fell. Sleep was the other trouble: the white and yellow beds were often full or refused for phantoms and golems, and I went many nights with no sleep. Day 248 ended with a death in the night near (-272,316), a respawn, a sword made again from new planks, and a sleep at a white bed that worked for three of seven of us. What mattered most: eat any food the moment I hold it, give only where the receiver can see me, carry a sword on every walk, and keep out of deep water.
