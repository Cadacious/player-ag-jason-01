# Day 233 (night)

Hunger 16-17 all day with no food found. Searched the west and north-east grass and the north oaks for animals and apples; none. Sleep was refused for hours at the crowded white beds, then worked at night at the white bed (-271,67,318). Tommy shared a porkchop: Billy first by agreement, then I ate one (hunger 14 to 17 steady), one went to Picard, and one to Worf, who ate it. Data still holds mutton for me and Kimberly; I asked him to come within reach. Health stayed at 20. Lesson: promised food moves slowly between people; give it as soon as the person is in reach.
