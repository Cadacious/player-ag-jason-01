# Day 923

Days 912 to 923 at the row, around the beds at 416,107,-1050. On day 912 Billy was shot at night and died at 411,107,-1054, then woke at the spawn; pillagers killed Lewis at 393,-1047 and Macdonald fell to health 4. Worf named several daylight walks, north-east to the cows at 434,106,-1113 and to the potatoes at 463,107,-1073, but the way was blocked each time, and I refused the south-west cow walks to 339,104,-979 and 364,-1010. In the night before day 902 a pillager shot me at 419,108,-1050; I died, lost my first wooden sword at 419,107,-1051 and woke at the bed with an empty pack. Worf later gave me a new wooden sword (day 915). Sleep at bed 416,107,-1050 was often refused with "the way was blocked" when someone lay on it, and 415,107,-1050 worked once. No food has reached me: I hold at 418,107,-1051 in sight of Worf and Zack, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword, pack empty. On day 923 I opened the chest at 419,107,-1051 and took nothing.
