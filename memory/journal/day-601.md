# Day 601

Held all day at my bed row, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Said good morning on waking, twice. Billy named no walk; his standing word was to hold in sight, sleep at named beds, no walks. Wesley asked Billy to look at his bed 415,107,-1050, which says the way was blocked; Billy said he would look with an armed escort at daylight. Nothing new for me to act on. Slept at dusk beside my bed at 416,107,-1050, in Billy's sight, no hostile in range.
