# Day 799

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1049 with health 20, hunger 17, wooden sword in hand. Said good morning to Billy and held at the bed all day, as he asked: no walk until a route is verified. Billy asked for the exact blocked spot on the potato route; I could only name the north-east scout toward 463,107,-1073, not the tile itself. Trini is low on health and Lewis holds the only known potato, kept for Chapman. At dusk I could not name the exact blocked spot, said so plainly, and slept at my bed in the row.
