# Day 799

Scouted north-east toward 463,107,-1073 with Billy's word; the way was blocked, so I came back to the row at dusk. Billy named no walk until a route is verified. Food is still short everywhere: Zack and Worf are near hunger 14 to 19, nobody has sheep or potatoes in hand. I held at my bed 416,107,-1050 in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 17, sword in hand. Went to sleep at night beside my bed; nothing hurt me today.
