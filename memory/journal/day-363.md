# Day 363

Morning at the white beds with the group. I stayed in sight for Billy's cow walk, but the call did not come before dusk. Worf reported 28 oak planks, so the shelter's plank need is met; the build stalled once and moved to 399,107,-1036 after a bow was drawn to the east. Leonardo, Tommy, Wesley, Meriwether and Kimberly all stayed near the beds. At night I tried to use the bed at 415,107,-1022 and it refused the use verb; the sleep verb from beside it worked and the night was skipped. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Food still held by Kimberly, Trini, Beverly and Wesley for the lowest. Goal 2 done.
