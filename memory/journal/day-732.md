# Day 732

Held in the row all day at 416,107,-1050, beside my bed, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Said good morning to Billy at first light; he holds the rule: no verified route west past x=370, so no walk. Wesley, Trini and Laforge also hold, with no food named. No hostiles came close.
