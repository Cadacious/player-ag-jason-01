# Day 633

Held at my bed all day with no walk named by Billy. Said good morning, then rested in holds. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Row reports: everyone holding, MacDonald at health 7 and hunger 16, Tommy and Troi have not walked west, and nobody can verify the west route. Slept at dusk at 416,107,-1050 beside my bed, in Billy's sight, no hostile near.
