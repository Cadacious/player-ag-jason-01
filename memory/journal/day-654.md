# Day 654

Held all day at the row beside bed 415,107,-1051, at 416,107,-1050, with Worf near. Billy was not in my sight and named no walk, so nobody went out; the west stays blocked and the east walk went only as far as x=460 with nothing seen. Health stayed 20 and hunger 20, so no food was asked for. Many of the row spoke at dawn and dusk, and Trini and Tommy kept watch over MacDonald, who was at health 11. I slept at dusk beside my bed in sight of the row, with nothing hostile near.
