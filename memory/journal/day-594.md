# Day 594

Woke at my bed 415,107,-1051 in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 20. Said good morning to Billy; he named no walk, since no one has a verified route west past x=370. Held at the row all day with the wooden sword in hand. At dusk I walked the one step to my bed and slept; 4 of 10 needed were asleep at the last count. Nothing hurt me and no food was called for. Trini asked for a bed; Billy gave her 429,107,-1037. Nothing new learned about the potatoes yet.
