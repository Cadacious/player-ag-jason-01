# Day 1139

Held at my white bed 415,107,-1050 in Billy's row all day. Health 5, hunger 16, no food, wooden sword in hand. Nobody in the row has food, and no one has named a checked open cell past 423, so no one walked. Riker said he would survey the ground east. I said at light that I hold until two readers name a checked cell. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at the bed in sight of the row.
