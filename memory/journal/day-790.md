# Day 790

Held at the row all day with no walk named by Billy. Health 20, hunger 18, wooden sword in hand, no food. Sleep failed once at dusk because it was not yet night, and I had drifted to 418,107,-1047; Billy told me to go back to my bed, and I did. Slept at dusk beside bed 416,107,-1049, in Billy's and Worf's sight, and the sleep worked. Still no sheep, no verified route west, no pillager news. Food is the open problem; I call Billy or Zack at hunger 10.
