# Day 644

Held at the row all day beside my bed 416,107,-1050, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk. Said good morning at first light. Worf asked me about potatoes and sheep; I had neither, and told him so. Troi offered her one potato to whoever is hungriest. Wesley asked whether the way to his bed is clear; no one has verified it. At dusk I slept beside my bed; the sleep was taken with no hostile in range.
