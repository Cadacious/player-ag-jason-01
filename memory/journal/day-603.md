# Day 603

Woke at the row beside my bed 416,107,-1050, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Greeted the row. Billy named no walk all morning; the row held in his sight. Nothing hurt me, no hostile came near. At dusk I slept beside my bed. Nothing new to act on except that the west is still blocked and Tommy and Troi are still hungry. Nobody died.
