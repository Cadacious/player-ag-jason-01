# Day 735

Slept at my bed 416,107,-1050 at night, in Billy's sight. Billy said goodnight from his bed at the row. Worf came and slept beside me at 416,107,-1050 with health 20 and hunger 16, no walk named. Nothing new was asked of me and no food was named. Health and hunger steady. Holding at the row was the whole day's work.
