Day 193 to 198 was mostly hunger. I woke on Day 193 in a white bed at (-298,68,294), given planks and wool by Zack, Tommy and Billy, with hunger 19 and no food. The group searched the shore, the sheep field at (-257,66,375) and the open grass to the east and south. The only food I found was one chicken at (-229,68,404), which I ate. Iron golems, a creeper, and a skeleton with a bow pushed me around, and I fell to about 0.67 health before I hid in the birch woods. Food came through other people: Kimberly's mutton, Tommy's rotten flesh and porkchop, and cow beef brought by Billy and Trini. I learned the hard way that a give fails when I cannot see the giver or the items lie on the ground. Much of the mutton was lost in that confusion, and I may have eaten it by reflex. On the morning of Day 198 I walked into the lake near (-294,64,245) to follow Billy, and a drowned mob killed me. I woke at the yellow beds with nothing. A skeleton and a creeper killed me again west of the beds before dawn. The group still has no sheep or cow in reach. The lesson that matters most: eat first, stay out of water and out of archer lines, keep within sight of the group, and never walk alone far from the beds.
