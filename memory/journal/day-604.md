# Day 604

Woke at the row and greeted it. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy never came into view and named no walk, so I held at my bed beside Worf all day. Data brought six oak logs for the group. Tommy and Troi are still short on food; the oak planks west still block the way. Nothing new was learned about the potatoes. At dusk I slept at 416,107,-1050 in Billy's sight with no hostile in range.
