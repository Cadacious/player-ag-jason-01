# Day 1074

Quiet day at my white bed 416,107,-1050 in the row. Health 20, hunger 19, wooden sword in hand, no food. No walk taken. Said I hold, and Troi, Zack and Billy each said they hold too. Nobody has named an open cell past 423, and I checked none myself. At night I slept in my bed with Worf, Zack and Billy in sight. Nothing new was learned today beyond the holding.
