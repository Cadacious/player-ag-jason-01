# Day 667

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1050 with health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Said good morning to the row and held for Billy's named call. Billy led a walk toward 436,107,-1042, then stopped it and called Zack, Picard and Riker back: nobody goes alone. No walk was named for me, so I stayed in the row all day. Night came quietly; Worf slept beside me. Slept at dusk at 416,107,-1050, in Billy's sight. Nothing new found; west still blocked, no food seen beyond the potatoes others hold.
