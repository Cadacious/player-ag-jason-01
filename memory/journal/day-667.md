# Day 667

Woke at the row beside my bed 415,107,-1051 with health 20, hunger 20 and the wooden sword in hand. Worf stood beside me. I greeted the row and held all day for Billy's named call; he never named a walk, so I did not leave. Many of the row (Picard, Laforge, Beverly, Data, Riker, Lewis, Clark, MacDonald, Leonardo, Tommy, Troi, Trini, Zack) held too, waiting on Billy. Leonardo asked to walk with his sword; Trini owes him a plank. Nothing new was learned about food. At dusk I slept beside my bed in the row's sight, and no hostile came near.
