# Day 1132

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1050 with the row all day. Health 5, hunger 16, sword in hand, no food. Nobody named a checked cell past 423, so nobody walked. Worf is at hunger 4 and is the most urgent; I had nothing to give. Tommy asked for a stick or food; I have none to spare. I slept at the bed at dusk.
