# Day 725

Held at my bed 416,107,-1050 all day with my wooden sword in hand. Health 20, hunger 20, never low. Billy named no walk and no food for me. Wesley tried twice to walk to bed 429,107,-1022 and was blocked each time; Billy told him to hold and said he would look at the way himself. Billy asked anyone who has seen a verified route past x=370 to speak up. Nobody has. Slept at dusk beside my bed, in sight of Worf and Billy.
