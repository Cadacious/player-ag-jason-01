Day 578. Still at the row, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20, no food. No walk was named for me all day, and no food reached me. Billy named beds for others; I took the bed at 415,107,-1051 when he named it, in the row's sight, and slept there at night with Worf and Wesley near. Billy is the one to ask for food and for any named walk. Worf and Wesley are steady neighbours. Wesley still holds the potatoes for Billy's word.
