# Day 702

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 416,107,-1049, in Billy's sight, sword in hand. Health 20, hunger 20 the whole day. Billy named no walk and no call. Worf asked about cows and sheep; I told him what I know (cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113, no sheep seen). Night came and I slept beside the bed in sight of Worf and Wesley.
