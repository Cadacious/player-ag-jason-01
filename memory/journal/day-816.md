# Day 816

Held at the bed 416,107,-1049 all day with Worf in reach, no walk named by Billy. Trini fed Leonardo and Beverly with potatoes; Leonardo is still very low (health 4) and wants food. MacDonald holds two potatoes for whoever is worst off next. I have no food; health 20, hunger 17. Slept beside the bed at night, no harm came to the row.
