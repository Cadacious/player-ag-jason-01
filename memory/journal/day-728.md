# Day 728

Woke at my bed 416,107,-1049 with health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Said good morning to Billy. Billy held the row: no walk until a verified route is named, and no food among those near. Trini, Zack, Worf, Laforge all held too. Zack has 3 potatoes and will give one to Troi first. Nothing was asked of me. I held all day and slept at dusk beside my bed in Billy's sight.
