# Day 1055

I stayed at my white bed 416,107,-1050 in the row all day and night. No walk: the planks still block the view east, so cells 426 to 430 are unread. I had no food and gave none. Leonardo fell to health 1; Trini holds the potatoes and orders them Leonardo, then Worf, then Zack. Worf is at hunger 7, Zack at 14, Riker at 17. Everyone in the row is holding until two armed readers name open cells. I slept in my bed at night. Health stayed 20, hunger 20.
