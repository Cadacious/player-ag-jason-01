# Day 648

Held all day at the row, at 416,107,-1050 beside my bed, in Billy's sight. No walk was named and no food reached me; Billy has still not spoken a call. Several people spoke of hunger: MacDonald at health 7 ate a potato from Zack, and Zack and Tommy hold the rest. I said good morning and stayed with the sword in hand. At dusk I slept beside my bed, nothing hostile near. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout.
