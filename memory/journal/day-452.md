# Day 452

Held at the bed row all day with Billy's group. Billy called no walk: he has not yet found a way to bring a sword into hand, and walks only with four in sight and two armed. I stayed counted, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Clark offered planks for a weapon; Billy asked him to keep them for now. Slept beside my bed 387,107,-1037 at dusk as directed.
