# Day 646

Held at the row all day beside my bed 416,107,-1050, in Billy's sight. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. No food reached me. Worf stood near me. Wesley said he would try my spot once at dusk. Picard and John each held at their beds for Billy's call. At dusk I said I would hold until morning and sleep here. Nothing hostile came near.
