# Day 670

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 416,107,-1050 with Worf near. Said good morning, then waited. Billy named no walk on day 669 or 670. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20 the whole time. The row checked in through the morning: Tommy, Picard, Trini, Troi, Data, MacDonald (health 11), Lewis, Riker and Clark (5 potatoes, offered to Data). Picard and Lewis asked again about sheep; nobody has seen any. Went to sleep at night beside my bed at dusk with Worf in sight. Nothing new learned except that the row keeps waiting for Billy's word.
