# Day 792

Held at the row beside my bed 416,107,-1050 all day, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 18, wooden sword in hand, no food. Billy named no walk on the first morning, then at the next morning named a short look east of the beds, with Worf and me as escorts. I agreed, but Billy's walk went out and back without me; I never saw him leave, so I stayed at the bed with Worf. The look found no food, sheep or news. Billy went to bed at dusk, and I slept in my bed in his sight. Sleep worked; days since sleep reset to 0. Nothing hurt me today.
