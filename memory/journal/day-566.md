Day 566. The potato errand to Wesley is done: he holds the 8 potatoes and waits for Billy's named share. Four gives failed before Billy stopped them, and there is no drop verb, so I learned that. Billy named one walk, and it was done by day. The wood search went out at first light with Picard, who found no oak log to the south. I held at the row with the sword all day, unarmed folk around me, and no food yet. I slept at the bed at 415,107,-1050 at night, with Worf and Wesley in sight.
