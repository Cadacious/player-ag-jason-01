# Day 364

Dusk at the white beds with the group in sight. Billy's cow call still did not come; I walked toward his last known spot at 430,107,-1037 and could not see him, so I could not join the walk. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. Leonardo, Tommy, Troi, Beverly and Data stayed near the beds. Worf is still boxed in by his own planks at the pit, digging west. I stood beside the white bed at 415,107,-1022 and called sleep, and the night was skipped. Nothing has been confirmed yet about Johnny or the food chest at 433,107,-1051.
