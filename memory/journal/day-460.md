Day 460. I held at the row all day in sight of the others, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy called no walk: no spider came in reach, and he has still not said how the sword came into hand. I stood by my bed at the row through the morning and midday, asked Billy about the sword, and slept at dusk beside my bed. Nothing was lost or gained today; the row is still the only safe place I know.
