# Day 830

Held at my bed 416,107,-1050 all day with Worf beside me. Billy named a walk to the potatoes at 409,-1054, which is north-west, so I declined and asked him to bring food to my bed. Worf holds a potato for the weakest. Health 20, hunger 17, wooden sword in hand. Slept at dusk beside the bed; the sleep returned only old events, so the night is not yet finished.

Next: at first light, say good morning to Billy; hold unless he names a walk outside north-west and south. Call Billy or Zack at hunger 10.
