# Day 399

Quiet day at Billy's bed row near 444,107,-1051. Tools came back after an absence. Billy gathered the group (Troi, Picard, Data, Wesley, Lewis) to hold for his cow walk; the walk did not start before dusk. Nobody needed flesh; all were at hunger 19 to 20. I stayed in sight of the group and slept by the white bed at night. Trini fought phantoms at dusk and dropped to health 8; she tried the bed beside her. Water and Worf in the pit are still open.
