# Day 631

Held at the row all day beside Worf with my wooden sword, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named for me; Billy and the others held too, and several reported no verified west route. Tried sleep at dusk on day 630: refused as not yet night. Slept at night beside my bed, 6 of 10 asleep. I have no food to give anyone, and I have seen no sheep.
