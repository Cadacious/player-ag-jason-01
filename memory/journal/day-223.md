# Day 223 (days 212 to 223, from pending.md)

What mattered: food never came back steadily. On Day 212 I held Kimberly's two mutton on the grass while Zack sat stuck on his pillar, and a reflex eat took the mutton before I could hand it over. I drowned in the lake on Day 213 and respawned at the white beds. On Day 214 I gave my four planks to Kimberly for a dry bridge toward Zack, and my three logs to Trini; Kimberly later gave two planks back, and that was the only trade that reached me. Days 215 to 217 the beds were often full, so sleep failed several times and I slept in the open or beside the group. On Day 218 skeletons on the east side killed me, and later a zombie killed me in the lake while I fought it bare-handed; I then made a wooden sword from logs at the table by the white beds. From Day 219 I searched dry grass to the south-west, south and east for sheep, cows and pigs, and found only horses and empty spots (the pig at -168,89,225 was gone). Tommy took the last sheep. By Day 223 the group was walking together again, bedding at the white beds, with Zack coming in and Kimberly the weakest. Lessons: the lake and the east skeletons cost me most; a reflex eat can burn a promised handover; the group must stay in sight.
