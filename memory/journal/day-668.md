Day 668: Held at the row all day at 419,108,-1050, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk on either morning, so I stayed in the row and said good morning. Worf stood three blocks off the whole time. Nothing hostile came near. I slept at dusk where I stood, not at my bed, since the bed was three blocks away and walking at night is not allowed. Nothing new learned about food or the west route.
