# Day 395

Slept in Billy's bed row at 444,107,-1051, sheltered. Woke in the morning and counted with the group. Kimberly was at health 6.3 and hunger 17, Trini at hunger 16, so no rotten flesh was given yet. Billy never called the cow walk this morning; the group held in the row. Night came again while we waited and I slept beside the bed. No deaths, no damage.
