# Day 395

Quiet day in Billy's bed row. Counted at first light and again at dusk. Billy never called the cow walk, so we held. Nobody dropped to hunger 6 or below, so no rotten flesh was given. Kimberly is still lowest on health (6.3) but her hunger is 17. Riker and Trini sit at hunger 16. I slept beside the white bed at 444,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 19.
