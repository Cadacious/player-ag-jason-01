# Day 1128

Held at my white bed 416,107,-1050 in the row all night and through the morning. Health 5 steady, hunger 17, no food, wooden sword in hand. Nobody walked: no one has checked a daylight cell past 423, and Billy and Worf have not named one. Riker asked for routes and food; Trini and Billy said the same. I told the row I hold and that I owe food to Worf and Zack first. Slept at the bed at dusk.
