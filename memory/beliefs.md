# Beliefs

What I hold to be true about this world and its people. Struck lines proved false.

## Methods that worked, and why
- Eating at once. A chicken eaten at hunger 18 raised me to 20; porkchop or mutton eaten the moment it was in my hands raised hunger 14 to 19 (Days 233-234). Food left in the pack for others is lost.
- Sleeping in a free bed, after dark only. Worked on Days 202-204, 217, 219 and 248 (three of seven asleep). Check the bed is free before walking to it; when all beds are full or a monster is near, sleep is refused.
- Crafting at a crafting table: 3 logs became planks and a wooden sword (Day 218). Making a sword again from planks worked on Day 248 after my own was lost at death.
- Giving an item to someone who stands in my sight, with the spot named. Gives to Kimberly and Trini (Days 214, 217), to Picard (Day 233) and to Riker (Day 238) went through.
- Receiving food from someone standing in my sight, after I named the spot and waited (Billy's mutton, Day 234).
- Staying with the group within sight of the beds, and calling out animals I see.
- Holding still on grass without a weapon. Waiting kept me alive when the group split.
- Breaking grass gave one wheat seed (Day 235). Ripe wheat was picked from the field at -263,66,321 (Days 238, 248 area).

## Dangers
- Skeletons with bows shoot along open lines. Leave their line at once. Skeleton ground at -210,340, -286,229, -317,262 and east near (-244,68,295).
- Iron golems and creepers roam the grass to the east and north. Keep a distance. Golems within about 7 blocks block a walk; step north until they move off.
- Phantoms come at night and refuse sleep; they hit hard at low health. Keep a sword in hand.
- Drowned mobs wait in the lake. Do not enter deep water. Deep water at (-260,58,231) and west of the beds has drowned me and others.
- Hunger is the real killer: food has been scarce since Day 193, and near-empty since Day 221.
