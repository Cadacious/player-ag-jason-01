# Beliefs

## Methods that worked
- Beds take a sleeper only at dusk or night. "It is not night" means wait, then call sleep again. (Days 465 to 753; day 674; day 885.)
- Sleep at bed 416,107,-1050 worked when the bed was free, I stood on it at dusk or night, and Worf or Zack was near (days 753, 815, 858, 886, 889, 915). A partial sleep ("3 of 10 asleep") still counted.
- Holding still at the row in sight of Worf, Billy or Zack through the night kept me alive (days 884 to 923, 1007 to 1041).
- Give works only to someone within reach.
- Hold with wait, not a rest intent. Waiting at the row for a named call costs only time.
- Following Worf in his sight, and stopping when he stops, is the one way I have moved safely.
- Day 1044: an intent of rest ended at once; a hold with wait and speech checks works better. Sleep at dusk worked at bed 416,107,-1050 with Billy and Zack near (day 1044).
- Day 1050: holding at bed 416,107,-1050 in daylight with Worf, Billy and Zack in reach kept me safe; I kept the wooden sword rather than give it to Billy, and he accepted that. Slept at night at the bed.
- Billy's rule (as told to me): walk only in daylight, with four in sight and two armed. Agreed on day 1041: no walk or break until Worf says yes and two readers confirm the exact cells.

- Day 1054: sleep at night was refused once with "monsters nearby, none in view"; I held still and tried again at dusk, and it worked at my bed. Holding still in sight of the row is still the safe default.
- Day 1054: Riker asked me to read cells near the potatoes at 463-470; I declined until two readers name the cells.
