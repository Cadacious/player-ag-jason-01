# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Eating a chicken as soon as I killed it. Hunger went from 18 to 20.
- Sleeping in a bed at night. Beds made the nights safe, and the white and yellow beds both worked.
- Crafting at a crafting table with planks and sticks: a wooden sword came out of it.
- Staying in a group near the beds and calling out what I see.
- Walking to a named person with goto, and waiting in their sight, so they can hand me things.

## Dangers
- Skeletons with bows shoot along open lines. Leave their line at once.
- Iron golems and creepers roam the grass to the east and north. Keep a distance.
- Drowned mobs wait in the lake. Do not enter deep water.
