# Beliefs

- Beds take a sleeper only at dusk. Using a bed by hand in daylight is refused. Standing beside the bed and calling sleep works.
- Bed 387,107,-1037 is mine by Billy's correction. 388,107,-1037 is cobblestone, not a bed.
- Walking in the dark is dangerous. Stay in Billy's sight.
- Swords stay in the pack; no one has found how to bring one into hand.
- Give works only to someone within reach.
