# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Eating at once: a chicken (hunger 18 to 20), and a porkchop or mutton eaten the moment it was in my hands (hunger 14 to 19, Days 233-234).
- Sleeping in a free bed after dark, at the white or yellow beds. Worked on Days 202-204, 217 and 219. Walk to a bed only after checking it is free. When every nearby bed is taken, sleep fails (Days 215, 216, 221, 225-226).
- Crafting at a crafting table: 3 logs became planks and a wooden sword (Day 218).
- Giving an item to a person who stands in my sight, with the spot named. Gives to Kimberly and Trini (Days 214, 217) and to Picard (Day 233) went through.
- Receiving food from someone standing in my sight, after I named the spot and waited (Billy's mutton, Day 234).
- Staying in a group within sight of the beds, and calling out animals I see.
- Holding still on grass when I have no weapon. Waiting kept me alive when the group split.
- Breaking grass gave one wheat seed (Day 235), which I gave to Wesley.
