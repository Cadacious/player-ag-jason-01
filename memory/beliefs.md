# Beliefs

What I hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

- Travel out and back to a far field takes most of a day; start the return early.
- Dusk comes fast; the bed row is the safe place to sleep with the group.
- The cow field (364,-1010) can hold bows and endermen; go there only in daylight, with company.
- Melon is shared with whoever is weakest, in sight.
- Johnny was reported found and safe by Picard on day 330; not yet confirmed by me.
- A sleep attempt may count only a few sleepers at first (4 of 10 on day 330); repeat it near the group.
