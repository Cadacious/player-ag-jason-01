# Beliefs

## Methods that worked
- Beds take a sleeper only at dusk or night. "It is not night" means wait, then call sleep again.
- Sleep at bed 416,107,-1050 (or 415,107,-1050) worked when the bed was free, I stood on it at dusk or night, and Worf or Zack was near. A partial sleep still counted. (Old row; not tried since day 1160.)
- Holding still in sight of an armed group through the night kept me alive: in the row (days 884 to 923, 1007 to 1154), and at the tables (days 1160 to 1173, with Samwise, Odo, Uhura and others).
- Hold with wait and speech checks, not a rest intent. A rest intent ended at once (day 1044).
- Following a leader in his sight, and stopping when he stops, is the one way I have moved safely.
- Crafting in sight of others works: I made jungle planks from Uhura's logs, then a wooden sword at the table at -948,78,-3012 (day 1171).
- Billy's rule (as told to me): walk only in daylight, with four in sight and two armed; no walk until two readers confirm the exact cells.
